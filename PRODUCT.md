# The Last Acquisition

## Register

brand

## Users

Dalmo prepares a home museum-gala murder mystery. Thirty guests read introductions on their phones before the party and use printed packets during play. Several guests are over 60.

## Product Purpose

A complete, editable party kit with host-blind murderer selection, readable printed assets, and a simple download homepage.

## Brand Personality

Theatrical, scholarly, mischievous. Museum documents should feel like objects from the gala. The homepage follows dalmo.ai, hiredalmo.ai and integral.dalmo.ai.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, faux luxury cards, tiny type, recovered-PDF line breaks, ornamental clutter and prewritten character quips.

## Design Principles

- Design for actual use: introductions sent as images, cuttable cards, room signs read from across a room.
- Use the user's 2024 invitations and 2025 round packets and props as production references, without copying their copyrighted art or text.
- Preserve host blindness and the original branch logic.
- Inspect rendered pages before releasing a build.
- Keep preparation straightforward and all source editable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

High-contrast book type. Body text at least 14 pt; introductions 16–20 pt. No font installation, phone mechanics or QR codes required during play. Costume suggestions remain optional.
