# Playtest and changes — spoiler-safe

The original 15/15 innocent solve rate and difficulty2/5 were a warning that the mystery was too transparent. That result is historical, not the current release's difficulty certification.

The new case replaces the explicit three-clue elimination scheme with competing dishonest accounts, conflicting museum records and dated visual evidence. Innocent scandals can explain suspicious testimony. Source checks cover all thirty selected-role routes without printing a suspect matrix for players.

Sixteen fresh persona assessments covered two scripted sixteen-character casts, eight readers per cast, including a core and an optional murderer route. **14/14 innocent assessments still identified the intended murderer**; the two murderer personas' strategic votes are excluded. Difficulty averaged 2.69/5 (range2–3/5). This remains unanimous AI accuracy and does not prove the mystery is sufficiently challenging for humans.

Feedback led to distinct Opportunity deflections, more innocent salon contacts, private innocent-cover-story guidance, character-specific Coming Clean errand resolutions, neutral rereads and dated visual comparisons. The structured investigation notes were removed. Covers, highlighted relationships, personal hunt hints, STOP panels, one question page per act and larger foldable cards improve practical handling.

Current packets have twelve pages: safe cover1; introduction2; private briefing3; hunt4; Motive5–6; Opportunity7–8; Method9–10; loose ballot11; Coming Clean12. Animal identity is memorized and the slip returned immediately. All three voted suspects read before any conditional animal fallback.

These were asynchronous readings with persistent transcripts, not live human parties. They do not measure listening, recall, physical hunting, accidental page turns or actual timing. A physical proof and human rehearsal remain unmeasured. Detailed raw feedback stays in spoiler/source material.
