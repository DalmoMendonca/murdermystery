Difficulty tuning rationale Every core suspect has a real motive and plausible opportunity before branching. The murderer gains one branch-specific contradiction or physical trace in Act III; innocent suspects gain a corroborated alibi or benign explanation. The killer should therefore rise above the field only for players who integrate motive, timing, and the late evidence rather than voting on motive alone. The fixed toxicology and camera clues deliberately do not narrow to a profession or a small subset.
