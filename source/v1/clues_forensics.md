Act I Scavenger Clues Cut apart by border/number without reading. Hide by card number using the facilitator placement table. The finder brings the clue to the central Evidence Table and earns one scavenger point.

1. Board Draft: The Naming Gift

A draft agreement proposes renaming the institution 'The Sterling Voss Meridian Museum' and gives the donor extraordinary influence over future acquisitions.

2. Geneva Shipping Manifest

A 1988 shipping record routes the Isfahan Star Bowl through a Geneva freeport under a shell company called Northstar Holdings. The owner line is blacked out.

3. Unsigned Warning

RETURN WHAT WAS TAKEN BEFORE THE STAR GOES DARK. No signature. The paper is ordinary museum stationery.

4. Embargoed Headline

A printout from Nadia Quill's notebook reads: 'THE MISSING DECADE IN THE VOSS COLLECTION - HOLD FOR 9:00 PM.'

5. Conservation Note

Rafael Gilt flagged modern adhesive beneath the Star Bowl's base, suggesting the object was remounted later than the public catalogue claims.

6. Legal Margin Note

On a draft donor agreement, someone has written: 'Permanent title does not cure disputed title.' The initials are H.C.

7. Auction Catalogue

A lot in an old auction catalogue is circled twice. In the margin: 'T.G. beat S.V. through proxy buyer. He will never forgive this.'

8. Student Painting Card

A label mock-up describes an 'early student work by Julian Brush, loaned by Sterling Voss.' Someone has written NO in thick pencil across the proof.

9. Education Budget

A draft budget moves money away from school tours and community programs toward a private donor lounge and new VIP entrance.

10. Camera Work Order

The Donor Salon security camera was intentionally taken offline for 'private donor meetings' during the gala.

11. 1989 Accession Page

An old accession notebook records one of Sterling Voss's earliest gifts with the note: 'Export papers promised - not yet received.'

12. Performance Sketch

A concept drawing by Imogen Muse resembles the gala's final reveal almost exactly, but the printed program credits the spectacle only to 'The Voss Foundation.'

13. Condition Photo

A setup photograph shows a faint crescent scratch already present on the cap of a replica service decanter used in the gallery display.

14. Crate Seal

A broken shipping seal from a recent crate is stamped NORTHSTAR LOGISTICS. Calvin Case has written: 'Why is this name back?'

15. Lighting Cue Sheet

The centerpiece lights flickered during rehearsal because someone changed the programmed cue after approval. The revised sequence is initialed R.B.

16. Private Menu Card

Sterling's service note says: 'Private cordial in silver coupe. No garnish. Deliver to Donor Salon before remarks.'

Fixed Forensic Evidence Place these five cards on the Evidence Table at the start of Act III.

F1 - TOXICOLOGY

Sterling Voss died from a fast-acting botanical cardiac toxin placed directly in his private silver coupe. The decanter and buffet food test clean.

F2 - TIME WINDOW

The toxin was most likely added between 6:40 and 6:49, after the cordial was prepared and before Sterling drank from it.

F3 - MISSING SAMPLE

A small reference vial of the same toxin is missing from a locked conservation materials cabinet. The cabinet was opened during gala setup; the old lock does not record who opened it.

F4 - CAMERA GAP

The Donor Salon camera was offline from 6:29 until after Sterling was found. The outage was intentional, not a technical failure.

F5 - SERVICE TABLE

Sterling's silver coupe stem appears to have been wiped. The service tray contains several overlapping prints, making touch evidence inconclusive by itself.
