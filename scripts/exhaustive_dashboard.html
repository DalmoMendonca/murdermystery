How to read this audit

These are attentive AI text readers with full recall, not simulated human solve-rate estimates. Five readers per murderer is a small descriptive sample. All 16 hunt clues are supplied to every reader; their physical discovery, table dynamics, acting, and visual interpretation are not tested. Readers have identical instructions and fresh context, rather than assigned personalities. Finale reviews use each reader’s top three suspects as a proxy for group voting. The published game is unchanged.
