## Nonnegotiable architecture

The shared discoveries and F1–F5 must be byte-identical for every culprit selection within a supported cast configuration, and preferably across cast configurations. The host chooses neither a forensic exhibit nor a culprit-specific staff statement. Role packets contain their I/G alternatives; the selected player privately uses G. Everyone else uses I. Fixed staff observations and other players' printed testimony cannot silently change when the killer changes.

A report saying the cyanide fragment fits Justin's lease in one world, Ella's portrait sheet in another and Elon's query in a third is incompatible. An unnamed picture depicting those three different documents is also incompatible. Moving that selection to the generator or a hidden host instruction does not fix it. The previous proposal is withdrawn, not deferred.

## Implementable discriminator: test accounts against fixed state constraints

The shared reports supply constraints, and role testimony supplies claims. A G branch must contradict a relevant constraint in a way that connects to the crime, not merely get a time wrong. An I branch must have a physically possible explanation that leaves a genuine scandal and, where appropriate, residual suspicion.

Use several different axes:

- **Source state:** a claimed liquid retained in the open-bottom copy is physically impossible. A closed-base object retaining liquid was not that copy. This identifies a false source explanation, not automatically its thief.
- **Carrier state:** an intact miniature neck/body cannot produce the joining historical-neck shard. A person who claims the wrapped blue shard came from their broken favor has misidentified or concealed its source.
- **Delivery state:** clean batch/food and donor-specific contamination reject a claim that contamination was entirely a shared catering issue. A substantive admitted intervention inside the target vessel matters more than exterior dust.
- **Sequence:** the fixed glass photographs constrain a claimed completed toast, but gaps prevent them becoming an exact alibi. A claim that one's act was after Casey filled the glass needs to fit the fixed collection/pour order.
- **Knowledge:** a guest's claim that they learned the retained-contents warning only after service can be compared with a fixed collections exchange. This is useful when it supports another material act; advance knowledge alone is only access.

Do not use all five in every G speech. Do not make thirty players recite “the bottle held liquid” and thirty I branches supply perfect certificates. The role dossiers must choose different combinations arising from their work, while sharing exactly the same object-state facts.

## What fixed reports can and cannot prove

With an identical evidence set and unchanged innocent accounts, switching only one person's G speech necessarily introduces the selecting information through that speech. This is not inherently unfair: murder mysteries often turn on a false account. It becomes a tell when the selected speech alone receives the crime props, loses corroboration or uses a repeated stock excuse.

Therefore audit every I/G pair for comparable consequential scene participation, different but substantive defenses and information released at the same dramatic time. The distinctive G inconsistency must become consequential because of Method findings, not because the G card visibly contains the only bottle story. Some innocent accounts should remain materially suspicious at the end.

Fixed chemistry cannot identify a named culprit by itself. Shared tissue cannot identify a person. A testimony contradiction can support 8 only if it connects source, deliberate intervention and target; “one person remembered wrong” cannot. None of the briefs above constitutes conclusive proof before it is fully staged and blind-tested.

## Audit of the ordinary-favor accident alternative

This alternative is host-blind compatible: every world can share the same original-neck fit, miniature-size exclusion and intact contaminated miniature. It is substantially better than selecting a personal paper fit and removes the shelf-relocation signature. The supported inference is precise: the recovered cyanide-bearing shard cannot be the ordinary miniature neck described in the selected guest's accident story.

It does **not** by itself establish that this guest broke the recovered shard. “I broke my own favor” could refer to another ordinary shard that was cleared away, while someone else broke the original. Unless the testimony links their accident to a particular observed bundle or shard through a natural scene consequence, the fixed report rebuts a possible interpretation, not necessarily their actual statement. Recovered poisoned miniature intact does not establish it was their personal favor either; everyone had common stock. Avoid silently equating three unlabeled things: their alleged favor, the recovered carrier and the recovered fragment.

Even with a real linkage, a guest could have innocently wrapped a shard already lying in mixed waste. A false accident account plus source connection can reach 6, but needs an independently grounded target-control or delivery discrepancy to support 8. Generic access and motive do not close this gap. Do not restore an incidental glass lift merely to obtain the second half.

G-only breakage would remain a recognizable repeated branch signature across thirty variants. Reduce the tell by placing several **different material disputes** in the permanent common scenes and by giving the selected account the same real scandal documentation as its I version. A few actual innocent favor accidents can exist naturally, with their own consequences, but adding thirty accidents or synchronized fragment certificates would repeat the failed architecture. Fixed reports may record separate ordinary miniature debris as well as the joining original fragment; they cannot assign the original fragment to the selected name.

The original/miniature size difference must also be believable to a hurried participant before Method. If the shard is visibly from a much larger bottle, a personal favor accident is a poor cover that an ordinary observer would reject in Act II. Use a small neck shard whose decisive curvature/dimensions require comparison, with text explaining that comparison at Method. Do not claim the recovered original body could itself pass for a tiny favor.

Recommendation: use the accident account as **one prototype's material contradiction**, not the universal G solution. In the next prototype, stage one independent observer of the consequential disposal interaction without describing the final recovery location. Keep that observation fixed in both I and G scenes, make their accounts of the item physically testable, and separately supply target control arising from the same conflict. If that cannot be done while preserving a natural I account and fixed witnesses across worlds, stop at a suspicious source case and record the design failure. It is a promising constrained experiment, not demonstrated random-thirty architecture.
