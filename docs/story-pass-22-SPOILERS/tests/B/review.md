Twenty-two people confessing misconduct in orderly rounds is conspicuously game-like. The final round systematically produces exculpatory paperwork for almost everyone, while Justin repeats an unsupported prop mistake. That makes him feel selected by narrative structure. I deliberately base the accusation on his two physical admissions rather than that contrast.
