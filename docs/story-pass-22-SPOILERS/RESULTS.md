## Structural failures retained

- The selected speaker alone supplies the source-object disposal plus particular-glass conjunction. This risks an authorial spotlight rather than a natural deduction.
- Innocent wrongdoing frequently supplies motive and general access, without a plausible causal crime hypothesis. Serious misconduct alone does not create strong murder suspicion.
- Multiple guests lift the same glass to retrieve papers. Different dialogue does not make that repeated action natural.
- Final speeches disproportionately supply documentary cleanup, while the selected person's shorter unsupported copy mistake stands out.
- Fragment and contaminated miniature remain anonymous. They demonstrate a possible mechanism more strongly than an individual's identity. An innocent mistaken handler plus another poisoner remains possible.
- All 30 guilty variants are not ready: only three prototypes are eligible; other 27 inherited routes remain unvalidated and known defective.
