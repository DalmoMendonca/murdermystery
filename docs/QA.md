# Release blockers

- Every eligible murderer has a coherent motive and the three required physical conditions.
- Single clues and clue pairs retain multiple plausible core suspects. Only the complete combination identifies the selected murderer.
- Optional-role absence never removes an essential witness, report, turn or evidence path.
- Animals are memorized and returned immediately to a closed box, never retained or returned to the draw bowl. Unused A animals are excluded from B; optional animals never enter B.
- Host blindness is preserved: no animal/name record and no pre-vote identification.
- Private selection labels say IF INNOCENT / IF MURDERER. Read-aloud boxes contain safe statements only. No active play page includes a confession or actual murder narration.
- All attending IDs ask and answer every round. The question catalog contains every character's tailored questions. Collect exactly one assigned receipt per guest.
- Reports are read at fixed gates: F1–F2, then F3, then F4–F5. Missing scavenger discoveries cannot make the murder unsolvable.
- Keep finales on the host tray until ballots are collected. Only the murderer opens their finale; other envelopes stay closed.
- No numbered ages, old names, prohibited topics or invented animal names in current generated assets. No raw fonts in downloadable ZIPs.
- All pre-party introductions are single-page image-friendly artifacts with large type, immediate relationship context, ACTING TIPS and optional COSTUME SUGGESTIONS, without page numbers or quips.
- All PDF pages are rendered. Inspect every distinct image; exact raster hashes establish identical duplicates. No clipping, bad wrapping, orphaned headings, broken glyphs or unintended spill pages.
- Evidence A/B pairs are equal, aligned, individually cuttable and neutrally labeled for blind assembly.

Run `python scripts/build.py`, `python scripts/check_content.py`, `python scripts/rehearse.py` and `python scripts/verify.py`. Visual review remains a separate required step. Record actual review coverage in RELEASE_QA, package after documentation updates, and compare live ZIP hashes after deployment.

Human playtesting and physical printer proof have not been performed. Do not describe simulations as a party playtest.
