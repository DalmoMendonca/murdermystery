# QA checklist

Treat these as release blockers:

- No core character's innocence depends on an optional guest attending.
- Every eligible murderer branch has a coherent motive, opportunity, cover story, and evidence path.
- Innocent routes remain suspicious enough to preserve uncertainty.
- Murderer selection stays host-blind.
- Player-facing materials never expose core/secondary/tertiary labels.
- No pre-party sheet reveals any relationship with Sterling Voss.
- Clues do not require phones.
- Act I clues enrich the mystery but missing scavenger finds cannot make the murder unsolvable.
- No banned content enters motives, jokes, red herrings, or backstories.
- Printables have no clipping, overlap, broken glyphs, or unreadably small type.
- Final ballots/confessions work for any eligible murderer.
- Optional-role absence cannot break the main deduction chain.
