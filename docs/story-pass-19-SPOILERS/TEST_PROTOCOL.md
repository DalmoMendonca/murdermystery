# Round 19 prospective test protocol — spoilers

Written before freezing or dispatching any Round19 blind reader.

Use the originally random Round15 selections for a controlled story comparison: A is the full thirty-person cast; B and C are the twenty-two confirmed roles. Each receives a fresh context-free reader and the same eight-stage order. A separate fresh D repeats B's exact story releases with the original unanchored 0–10 scoring instruction. Do not replace a failed reader or rescale scores after seeing results.

The story releases now omit internal renderer-only asset names. These transcript readers receive text equivalents of all essential observations, not actual photographs; record that limitation. A physical exhibit review must separately inspect generated art and the final kit.

For A/B/C use the previously declared scale: 0 excluded, 2 little evidence, 4 motive/opportunity with little connection to the crime, 6 multiple specific grounds for a credible investigation, 8 strongest material contradiction with reasonable alternatives, 10 effectively conclusive. D gets only the original request to score suspicion from 0 to 10. No reader is told the selected character, thresholds, prior rankings, intended plot, or desired result.

Each stage must be read using the gated release tool, scored for every attending name, saved, and validated before reading the next stage. Retain every raw input, original result and release journal. Inspect hash provenance before export and unblinding. Never edit a frozen source or previously submitted result.

Assess the requested numeric targets and the reasoning: no fair culprit identification before Act III; at least one third strictly above 5 after Act II; selected murderer the unique final leader; three or four final rivals at 5–6. Ask readers for the earliest fair identification, competing cases, implausible conduct, writing tells and remaining causal gaps. A numerical pass with contrived storytelling is rejected. Supplemental D measures scoring sensitivity; report it even when it undercuts the main trials.

If the draft fails prefreeze coherence, do not dispatch it just to collect another heatmap. Fix and document the candidate before its first freeze.
