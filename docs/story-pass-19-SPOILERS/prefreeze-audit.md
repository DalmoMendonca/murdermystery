## 1. The central inference is too strong

The service collage visibly shows a wrapped empty glass, an unwrapped empty glass, an empty glass being collected onto a tray, and punch being poured into it on the tray. The empty interior is legible. Those are useful illustrations of the stated 6:30, 6:32, 6:44 and 6:46 sequence. No clocks or timestamps are visible in the images; their times derive from Casey's signed account, not independently readable photographic metadata.

Empty at 6:32 and empty at 6:44 **does not imply empty at every intervening moment**. A guest could have seen it temporarily containing liquid, then another guest could have removed that liquid before collection. That account requires another action, but not another murderer or an accomplice: there are many ordinary visitors and handlers. Nor would changing the glass's contents necessarily leave an independently reported record. The fixed authorial crime may say the target was empty when contaminated; the players have not thereby received proof that it never held liquid during reception.

F4's “It was first filled ... at 6:46” and timeline “First fill” are stronger than the pictured events. Casey was in the kitchen during the relevant interval. Casey can report **their own fill**, when they collected the glass, and what they observed at collection. They cannot simply certify that nobody filled and emptied it while they were absent. The explicit last sentence admitting that pictures are snapshots does not undo the earlier categorical first-fill claim.

Concrete correction: state “Casey filled it from shared punch at 6:46; the placement and collection images show an empty interior.” Treat a guest's earlier account as challenged, potentially mistaken or evasive, not conclusively disproved. Do not introduce continuous surveillance merely to rescue it. If a particular guest makes a claim about **the pictured collection event itself** (“I watched Casey collect it full”), the image can contradict that narrower claim; first establish why that guest honestly claims to have watched that event. This is a possible bounded correction, not permission to assign thirty identical collection witnesses.

The current Method retreats largely concede an empty-throughout interpretation the evidence has not established. Paige specifically asks the right question: “Was the glass empty throughout, or is that the collection picture?” Her next “You have the sequence. Fine” yields to evidence that still has a twelve-minute gap. Reject that forced concession. Al's remembered weight and Ella's possible reflection are competing explanations, not proof of knowing deceit. Even an established false observation would implicate credibility, not identify the cabinet thief or poison carrier.

## 8. Two viable directions and their failure limits

### A. A bounded common-state revision

This is defensible for a best-explanation game if the state claim is demoted from a proved lie to a contested, self-serving account. It need not provide courtroom certainty. Casey can **state** that the 6:46 pour was the first fill they performed or understood to occur; their absence and the photographs' limits stay explicit. Do not make their interpretation an independent observation of the whole interval.

Ask the same visible, ordinary question of every guest: what was service doing when you used reception, and what did you do that affected it? That makes service an expected subject instead of a selected guest's unexplained liquid detail. It also permits honest “I didn't check” answers. Asking about service cannot by itself make an unequivocal liquid observation believable in all thirty guilty voices.

Give three or four active innocent speakers sincere readiness or liquid misreadings for reasons already belonging to them. Suitable candidates are Faye conflating a later tray/portrait sighting with her earlier visit, Dada confusing what was prepared for his canceled rehearsal with actual donor service, Artie relying on an assistant's program readiness, and Elon interpreting a carefully arranged donor place as completed hospitality. The first two need the specific later photo or rehearsal plan cited, not a vague new explanation; the latter two can admit an assumption without receiving a full alibi. Keep PROP separate and do not add a new staged substance casually. A director's readiness assumption need not mean he actually saw liquid.

At least some innocent misreadings must include a specific liquid impression if G still universally says “punch.” Otherwise G is still the only exact-liquid claimant, with innocence receiving conveniently weaker “ready” language. The innocents' clarifications can mitigate a particular confusion without certifying their entire night. They may retain other lies and real target access.

The selected G's Method response must then remain less satisfactory **for a concrete reason beyond being selected**: a retained instruction contradicts the action minimized, a claimed staff cover did not exist, or their supposedly brief errand included independently supported interference they concealed. Brie/Monet/Barb already have related records and conduct; professions do not need to forget basic service. Conversely, simply admitting “I assumed ready” resolves the original claim about as well for G as for the innocents. The present universal retreats therefore do not supply that differential evidence.

Failure limits: this remains fragile for Hugh, Saul, Sue, Elle and Justin, whose private errands do not naturally require confidently naming punch. If their strongest honest answer is “I wasn't looking at service,” do not force a drink lie into it. The three-to-four innocent design cannot be treated as a scoring quota or a promise of numerical breadth. This is an economical revision worth a fresh unanchored reading, not grounds to freeze now.

A genuinely varied alternative can preserve all thirty selectable culprits and eight releases, but it needs **additional independent record content**, not merely new synonyms using the current five reports. The current shared assets can verify objects, service moments, and laboratory results. They do not independently refute thirty different individuals' claims. Those records challenge concealed interference. The documents must be independently quoted or shown in the exhibit, not merely volunteered by the selected guest at stage 8. 2. **Object/task misdescription:** Chip, Cary, Frank, Al, Elle, Dada and Minnie describe the thing they moved, substituted, inspected or canceled as unrelated to target service. These families can establish that a guest is lying about consequential conduct already linked to reception or source response. They do **not** automatically make every selected variant the best murderer case. In quieter roles such as Hugh or Saul, a disproved business cover may still be weaker than Brie's documented cover removal and target handling. The variant must connect its contradicted account to the actual vulnerable target or source route, with a plausible reason for the concealment. If the evidence only reveals financial dishonesty, reject that variant as under-supported rather than escalating its confession or granting everyone else an alibi.

This alternative is viable because the disclosures are existing character misconduct corroborated by records, not arbitrary new poison labels. It is more work than option A, and some variants may require rejection or stronger individual opportunity authoring. It avoids thirty uniform liquid claims and lets different delayed facts challenge different covers. Its failure mode is turning every record into another forensic confession or assuming “liar about papers” means “murderer.” Retain the physical chain and serious rival acts as separate parts of the case.

Recommended next choice: try the bounded common-state revision only for variants whose service minimization has a believable purpose, with shared questions and a few natural innocent misreadings. Use the record-based cover families for the others. Do not promise that every existing variant is accepted before its independent counterfact and cumulative case have been written. The exact fixed crime and thirty selectable characters can remain; unconditional acceptance of all thirty current scripts cannot.
