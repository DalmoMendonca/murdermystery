# Source-object alternative: independent audit

**This is worth prototyping because it addresses the actual attribution problem. It is not ready to freeze as a thirty-role mechanism.** A prior admission of moving the subsequently identified source, combined with already disclosed target contact, is materially stronger than a lie about an invoice or a missing lease. The uncertainty then concerns what the guest knowingly did with the crime objects, rather than whether unrelated dishonesty should count as murder evidence.

I read the service and records designs, the object designs, and `alternative-architecture-review.md`. The service designs have substantial target-response connections, especially deliberate uncovered service and suppressed hazard response. Most records designs have an independent contradiction but a weak murder nexus; Hugh, Saul and Justin are explicitly rejected as sufficient cases. The object designs likewise reject Chip, Elle, Al and Cary under their present records. A physical alternative should be judged against that weakness, not against an imagined set of thirty already viable document mechanisms.

## The useful inference

The proposed Opportunity account contains two substantive actions before the final evidence: relocating a loose object believed to be the replica to the space behind reception, and interfering with the actual donor target. Stage 7 identifies the recovered object as the original through its closed base and historical receiving comparison, with the neck join and cyanide findings establishing the source-to-carrier-to-target route. The guest's account now includes a crime source at its recovery location and the poisoned target during the vulnerable interval.

That is a best-explanation argument. It is not an identity label on the carrier, and does not require a witness to the theft. The admission concerns physical conduct, which can be innocently misunderstood or deliberately misdescribed. A guest cannot be eliminated merely because they admit the same lesser action more candidly.

The linked-object premise needs care: “I moved a bottle behind reception” does not automatically mean “I moved the recovered bottle.” The description must be specific enough to compare with the recovered object without becoming an early confession to handling poison. Object position and an ordinary visual description can narrow the account. Do not add a unique wrapper, private scratch, selected fingerprint or recipient tag to close the remaining uncertainty.

## Early suspicion versus a branch tell

This will make the selected guest a serious suspect at stage 6. Three staff handling a known replica elsewhere does not make everyone equally implicated in relocating an unexplained bottle to the exact recovery area. That is an earned lead if it emerges from the admitted actions. It should not be disguised in the report as broad stage-6 equality or forced into a prescribed late numerical curve.

There is also a genuine branch risk. If twenty-seven innocent guests discuss documents while one guest alone suddenly gives a precise bottle-relocation account, a reader can select the guest by narrative prominence before identifying the object. Varying adjectives would not repair that. The guilty account must answer the same ordinary question as the innocent account and arise from the guest's active dispute. It should not stack source relocation, miniature opening, source damage and target handling into one extraordinarily helpful speech.

The architecture can tolerate one guest having a distinctive crime-relevant act; mysteries depend on such acts. The test is whether readers explain the eventual physical comparison and the source/target conjunction, or merely say that the unusual bottle speech must be the guilty branch. Preserve the staff's consequential workflow accounts and the service rivals' real intervention, rather than adding bottle touching to twenty-seven innocents for camouflage.

## Innocent misidentification remains a serious countercase

Another guest could steal the original at 6:12, leave it loose, and later use poison from it while the speaker innocently relocates it. Alternatively, the speaker could move a replica that someone later returns or replaces, leaving the recovered original in that space. These interpretations require no accomplice: an unwitting mover can coexist with a sole murderer.

The physical comparison defeats an object description only to the extent that the compared bottle is the one moved. Even that does not prove knowledge of contents. The guest could have mistaken a closed-base original for a similar replica without looking underneath. Moving the donor glass afterward could still be incidental handling. Source handling plus target handling nevertheless gives a substantial cumulative case; the innocent countercase becomes less economical when it requires a second unobserved source journey and a separate poisoning visitor.

Do not call that innocent sequence impossible. Do not make the replica's receiving photograph continuously account for it afterward. Do not treat the existence of three legitimate staff handlers as proof that all other handling must be guilty. The known replica chain should establish what was normally done and who knew the distinction, while leaving later departures possible.

## Midpoint breadth and final rivals

The alternative can sustain meaningful breadth without distributing source possession to every guest. At stage 5, an unidentified blue recovered object, the forced theft and the donor's exposed interval establish a suspicious location. Chip's kit and withheld warning, Cary's receiving work and lever, Barb's hazard-response suppression and waste-area movement, Brie's direct target handling and refusal of coverage, and Monet's extended manager absence supply different concrete inquiries. Art and Reed's research requests supply additional source knowledge without themselves proving access.

At stage 7, use three particularly durable rivals:

- **Chip:** source knowledge, withholding the condition warning, tools and target-area kit; the real crack and date misconduct explain those acts but do not exclude a later theft.
- **Cary:** physical receiving knowledge, an available forcing tool and reception return; intact earlier receiving sorts the objects but does not account for subsequent movements.
- **Barb or Brie:** Barb connects source response with the disposal area; Brie connects deliberate lack of cover with actual target handling. Either remains substantial even when the other is selected.

When Chip, Cary or Barb is selected, retain the other two and Brie. When Brie is selected, retain Chip, Cary and Barb. For other selections, the same four permit at least three rivals. Their strength need not be numerically equal. The final selected case can lead because it joins an admitted source relocation to the actual target, while rivals retain plausible access and misconduct of their own.

This works only if those rival actions remain legible at the midpoint. A source comparison cannot compensate for thirty bland innocent alibis. Existing dates, clear food tests and replica descriptions must resolve particular routes or lesser acts, not absolve whole people.

## Jobs and reasons for relocation

Staff workflows are strongest: conservator, handler, security, service, event producer and director can plausibly intervene in a misplaced display object. Frank and Dada can plausibly interact with what they believe is an exhibition or rehearsal object, but their reason for putting it behind reception needs a real physical purpose. A photographer may move something out of a portrait arrangement. These are different actions, not automatic permission to hide museum property.

For a dealer, collector, curator, attorney, scholar or trustee, removal may be deliberate interference with a disputed display or a self-serving tidying choice. It should remain questionable. It need not be made respectable to work as a murderer's cover. Their reason must explain the actual relocation, not merely their presence near papers.

The least natural fits are a reporter, educator, activist and local historian. Their established objectives concern evidence, people, public copy and access. Claiming to protect a loose replica after a poison theft may be opportunistic, but repeated “making room for my papers” becomes the old universal sideboard problem with a bottle added. Reject any branch whose only plausible reason is an invented emergency, a new instruction from staff or an otherwise unmentioned fascination with the exhibit. Do not add thirty unnamed witnesses or permission conversations.

Thus a common physical crime can work across selectable worlds, but a common relocation excuse cannot simply be assumed to work across thirty jobs. Review each branch against its existing errand. The selected world needs one additional mover; it does not require thirty movers in one gala.

## Required fixed-object and release changes

1. The current replica is clear and the original dark blue. A lookalike dark-blue replica is an actual fixed redesign. Receiving text, art, prop descriptions and every innocent explanation must agree. Preserve the open bottom as the established replica distinction and the historical original's closed base.
2. Stage 5 must call the recovery a bottle pending comparison. If it already identifies the original, the Opportunity replica cover is contradicted before it is spoken. This also means stage 5 no longer independently proves the source's presence at reception; describe that uncertainty honestly.
3. A natural initial scene photograph can show an upright bottle without revealing its underside. A later documented examination can reveal the base and neck join. Do not crop out a visible identifying feature or make the first image artificially unreadable to enforce staging.
4. The open-bottom rule may be known in Hunt. Stage 7's new fact is the recovered object's construction and comparison, not a newly invented identification rule.
5. Establish what kind of space is behind reception before the guest uses it as an explanation. An ordinary temporary holding space is more credible than “safe storage” behind furniture. Any new layout fact must remain fixed across worlds; it cannot become a selected-only disposal convenience.
6. Keep the original theft at 6:12, target exposure at 6:32–6:44, filling from clean punch at 6:46, toast at 6:49 and gala death. Moving the bottle alone must not assign another person's crime-window whereabouts.

## Recommendation

Prototype this physical alternative before drafting thirty more speeches. Test one staff mover, one service or production mover, and one difficult nonstaff mover against the same exhibits and substantial innocent rivals. Justin is a useful stress test because his existing lease and recorder context strongly explains misconduct but does not naturally explain museum-object relocation.

Require readers to state the stage-6 conduct that made the guest suspicious, the stage-7 physical fact that changed its meaning, a viable innocent misidentification account, and why the selected cumulative case is stronger than at least three named rivals. Accept an earned early preference. Reject selection based only on the unusual bottle speech or an asserted impossibility of innocent source handling.

This alternative has a stronger causal core than the universal document-lie plan. Its remaining problem is believable action coverage across the thirty roles, not a need for more forensic certainty. Adopt it only after the difficult branches pass that test; otherwise retain it as a source-contact family alongside the genuinely consequential service covers.
