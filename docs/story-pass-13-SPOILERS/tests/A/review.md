The distinction between reception service, lighting override and security circuits is clear. An empty jacket and a marked prop glass credibly explain why an apparent-collapse performance need not poison anyone.

The murder still lacks an observable transfer step. We never hear how Ella covertly accessed a sealed cyanide bottle during a group preview, removed poison, carried it, and dosed the glass without detection. Common foil could accompany handling, but the paper in her clutch is not a unique tear match and does not by itself establish poison carriage. ## Presentation and voices

Introductions have entertaining occupational voices, especially security, auctioneer, caterer and critic. The later thirty speeches flatten into the same articulate, self-indicting voice. Repeated structures are: I committed a smaller wrong; Grant exploited it; here is a witty distinction; evidence explains my material or object; I accept responsibility. Many sound like polished deposition summaries by the same writer. Thirty equally cooperative confessions and numerous precise technical denials feel contrived for ordinary guests.

Ella's final line stands out stylistically because it alone retains incriminating physical possession while most other final speeches separate their scandal from poison. The audience can spot the unclosed red herring by editorial pattern. Explicit statements such as a coat not becoming a drink container also sound addressed to solvers rather than another character.

Barb says she personally disconnected the camera at checkpoint 06 although the work order says a staff technician did so. It can mean she ordered it, but wording should align.
