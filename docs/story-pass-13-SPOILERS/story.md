# Pass 13 — SPOILERS

This is an unpublished private-story revision. The sent invitations, public character copy, portraits and character sheets remain locked. The existing museum gala, thirty roles, secret-animal selection, three branching acts, hunt, ballot and Coming Clean procedure remain intact.

## What changed in the writing process

Game Narrative supplied the distinction between the crime history and the guests' experience of investigating it. Story Project supplied scene objectives, resistance and consequences. Genre Craft supplied the mystery clue ledger and the requirement that misleading evidence have an actual explanation. Its comedy module supplied escalation through a character's own attempted fix rather than detachable jokes.

Those methods are adapted to an existing party kit, not used to replace it with a novel scaffold. The planning scene units are in [beats/evening.md](beats/evening.md). Individual wants are in [goals.yaml](goals.yaml). Authored speech and ending source is in `dialogue.yaml` and `endings.yaml`; `investigation_copy.yaml` holds the corresponding branch structure. These files are separate from canonical PDF sources.

The dominant contract is a fair mystery. Comedy comes from status, bad bargains, professional vanity and failed attempts to conceal embarrassing facts. It does not excuse an impossible physical sequence. Every innocent has an unrelated suspicious act and an explanatory disclosure; the audit must distinguish that explanation from a genuine murder exclusion.

## Crime history

Grant is trying to turn a gift into control of the museum. Its ownership claim depends on an incomplete accession history; its gala presentation depends on staff and artists whose shortcuts and unpaid work are beginning to collide. He participates in the board discussion and responds to the collection dispute through his assistant. He does not personally blackmail or confront thirty guests in twelve minutes.

Meanwhile a real mounting defect stops the unveiling. The museum continues its ordinary reception. Guests move among the office, reading room, gallery and service area; the fault does not herd them into one confrontation. A canceled stage effect, camera exception and disputed catering authorization have separate causes.

In the selected culprit version, that character takes poison during the earlier acquisition preview and contaminates Grant's new glass on the reception sideboard. The glass is later collected onto a tray, filled with shared punch and delivered for the toast. Grant dies at the gala afterward. The bottle remains partly full, with hidden band damage. This is fictional case material; no chemical procedure or dose is specified.

Only the selected guilty version adds that character's physical encounter with the actual donor glass. That encounter is stated in Opportunity, before the final laboratory interpretation. Innocent glass contacts are limited: Al's mock toast and Brie's cleaning are real; Tess breaks a different rental glass, Cary delivers the donor glass wrapped, Dada handles a separate prop, and Minnie adjusts the setting without moving the glass. No world contains thirty guilty versions at once.

## Investigation experience

The hunt introduces the disputed gift, old ownership file, proxy purchase, altered credits, safety finding, service order, privacy exception and hazardous private acquisition. Guests share all sixteen discoveries. Nobody must find a particular envelope to have access to a necessary fact; the host supplies any missing ones. Existing hiding places and editable hints remain unchanged.

Act I gives cause of death and bottle disturbance, alongside motive testimony. Act II gives the service record and individual actions but does not yet clear shared food, drink and rehearsal equipment. Act III supplies the laboratory comparisons and condition record, then the final accounts. Players can reconsider the delivery route and the earlier actions before voting. Coming Clean supplies a confession or admission of the resolved scandal, not a previously withheld murder clue.

The ballot remains individual and tear-off. The three leading suspects read their selected Coming Clean branch. The murderer confesses afterward if not among them. Questions remain in each packet; there is no outside catalog or assumption that guests arrive in pairs. A host-facing turn checklist ensures everyone is heard once per act, without an added player rule guaranteeing testimony truth.

## Honest limits of this prototype

The common paper inside the glass is not a unique torn-edge identification. The source/glass/material combination is an intended comparative inference. Explaining a different scandal does not conclusively exclude murder; four late innocent arrivals do provide a source-window exclusion. The remaining cases require independent review rather than a clearance-metadata assertion.

The hazardous original and the traveling paper fragment may still feel too convenient. Speech variety, an early guilty fingerprint in prose, and the difference between broad access and true suspicion are also explicit review questions. A delayed lab report alone is not evidence that Act II will be balanced.

Three readers receive eight frozen releases independently. A uses thirty roles; B and C use the confirmed twenty-two. Culprits are uniform random selections from the attending cast without replacement across trials. Readers receive no solution, target scores, source files, prior drafts or finale. All results are retained. These are transcript plausibility tests, not human solve-rate estimates or visual PDF signoff.
