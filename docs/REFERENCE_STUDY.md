## 2025

Twenty character PDF books, host and inspector guides, invitation, ballot, slang sheet, fire report, three JPG evidence props and six announcement videos. PDF text was extracted and every PDF page rendered for study. Character guides have branded covers, clear round labels and prominent STOP instructions; rounds separate questions from innocent/guilty answers. The host guide separates preparation and timed announcements. Fire report, handwritten note, notebook and newspaper clipping act as distinct physical objects.

For 2026, retained release boundaries and handling cues. Public introductions remain separate from four-page private play packets and a separately sealed finale. Three mandatory hearings reproduce the references' useful question-and-answer structure: motive, whereabouts, then evidence. Every guest asks and answers in seat order; the host ticks an attendance roster, releases evidence at explicit gates and locks ballots before the confession. Read-aloud boxes are separated from private routing instructions. Clues have document-specific typography, useful physical dimensions and dashed cutting borders. Signs, exhibit labels, ballots and certificates each have a layout for their actual use. Neutral A/B cards stay paired, so names and sheet numbers guide blind assembly without revealing assigned branches.

The references also contain small type, repeated promotional pages and unused space. Those patterns were not carried forward. Every final 2026 PDF received separate rendered visual review.
