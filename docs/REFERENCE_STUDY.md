# 2024 and 2025 reference study

Inspected the local party folders under Desktop/Files/Parties. Reference files remain private local material and are not copied into this repository.

## 2024

Twelve JPG introductions use a navy-and-gold nautical frame, strong name hierarchy, short character bios and costume inspiration. Their strength is a recognizable party world and self-contained invitation suitable for forwarding. The 2026 introductions adopt that self-contained format using the museum's teal masthead, generous serif type and archival border.

## 2025

Twenty character PDF books, host and inspector guides, invitation, ballot, slang sheet, fire report, three JPG evidence props and six announcement videos. PDF text was extracted and every PDF page rendered for study. Character guides have branded covers, clear round labels and prominent STOP instructions; rounds separate questions from innocent/guilty answers. The host guide separates preparation and timed announcements. Fire report, handwritten note, notebook and newspaper clipping act as distinct physical objects.

For 2026, retained release boundaries and handling cues. Public introductions remain separate from two-page private packets; a STOP footer protects page two. Clues have document-specific typography, useful physical dimensions and dashed cutting borders. Signs, exhibit labels, ballots and certificates each have a layout for their actual use. Neutral A/B cards stay paired, so names and sheet numbers guide blind assembly without revealing assigned branches.

The references also contain small type, repeated promotional pages and unused space. Those patterns were not carried forward. Every final 2026 PDF received separate rendered visual review.
