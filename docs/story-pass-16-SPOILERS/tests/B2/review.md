## Concrete fixes

1. Supply an independent, person-specific intersection between the common favor and the transfer window: a photograph showing a distinctive preplanted mark, a contemporaneous witness to placing a bottle in waste, or a cap/label fragment tied to an already-established personal object. Avoid simply adding a culprit name to the laboratory result.
2. Give the theft a recoverable attendee link, or explicitly establish the transferer could use poison another person stole. Currently the dramatic forced-entry preparation is disconnected from attribution.
3. Resolve Al's uncapped favor concretely if he is intended as innocent: retain it in his invoice case and independently test it, or establish his contact occurred before opening the glass wrapper. A fragrance stain alone is insufficient. Likewise corroborate Brie's returned packet rather than relying on her own limited-wiping claim.
4. If Justin is intended as guilty, plant his sideboard action earlier and make the finale contradict an earlier factual claim. A favor moved beside the glass is common opportunity, and the last unresolved speech is an editorial signal. Give readers an inference they can state without referring to speech order or tone.
5. Attach times to Al's mock toast, Brie's wiping, Justin's gathering, and donor-glass identity. Proximity outside 6:32-6:44 may be harmless; presently readers must assume every scene belongs to the relevant interval.
6. Clarify whether receipts mean first entry and whether staff are outside the accusation set by game rule. If excluding staff evidentially, supply independent custody or location evidence for the relevant period.
7. Replace the synchronized confessional template with varied amounts of candor and role-specific vocabulary. Keep some explanations unresolved across all characters so the culprit is not singled out solely by lack of a tidy final rebuttal.
8. Trim corrective-document phrasing. Let the actual power wiring, cabinet damage, replica shape and cancellation timestamps do the explanatory work.

The route is intelligible and fairly prepared. The identity is not yet uniquely earned. I would distinguish a reasonable accusation from a solved mystery and accept more than one accusation unless additional evidence is available in play.
