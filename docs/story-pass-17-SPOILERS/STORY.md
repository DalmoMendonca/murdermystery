# Round 17 — isolated story experiment, spoilers

Not the production kit. The sent invitation and public character sheets remain immutable.

## Crime and investigation

Grant acquired an authentic bottle from the Eleanor Vale murder trial for *The Art of Murder*. The museum refused to exhibit the original and secured it inside a secondary case in a locked cabinet. The public display uses an empty replica. Grant incorrectly told security the original had been emptied. His pressure and that false claim explain a delayed response; they do not excuse it. When the auction condition contradicts him, security requests assistance.

The selected guest forces the cabinet and case, steals the original, and conceals it behind the reception sideboard after using a miniature favor bottle to carry poison to Grant's individual glass. This is an author-only causal description, not a preparation instruction. Guests all receive the same kind of fragrance favor in assorted art-glass colors. The common contaminated miniature establishes a route, not an owner.

A fragment breaks from the original's neck. The selected guest discards it in favor paper and describes the accident as breakage in the gift packet. Only that alternative guilty-world account is spoken in a given game. Thirty simultaneous bottle accidents are not part of the story.

The investigation first reveals a blue fragment in favor paper. Its source is unknown; assorted colored favors make an ordinary broken gift a possible explanation. Act III supplies the recovered original and the actual fracture comparison: irregular edges and a divided inventory stamp join. This changes the meaning of the earlier account. It is a materially stronger link than compatible chemical residue or a common manufacturer label.

## Release design

1. Introductions: unchanged public identities; stated scope is one playing culprit, no accomplices. No guarantee that printed innocent statements are truthful is added.
2. Hunt: sixteen independent objects and documents; gift program establishes ordinary favors, receiving record establishes secured original, disputes supply several kinds of motive. Envelopes remain optional enrichment rather than a required private catalog.
3. Motive evidence: unsigned gift conditions, death, and missing historical bottle. The first photograph must show the closed receiving case, not reveal the source's blue fractured neck prematurely.
4. Motive speeches: distinct misconduct and pressures.
5. Opportunity evidence: source theft, ordinary reception service, exposed glass, and unidentified fragment in favor paper.
6. Opportunity speeches: distributed disputes. The guilty account includes the gift-breakage cover story; no source-fracture identity has yet been established.
7. Method evidence: recovered original, physical fracture comparison, and test results. Al's recovered favor is tested separately; an empty target glass never counted as an alibi.
8. Method speeches: accounts of actual handling and their scandals. Compare the observations; a guilty-only lack of reassurance is not sufficient proof.
9. Accusations and Coming Clean: still require a later authored polish and every-culprit audit before integration.

## Specific failure risks to test

- A single broken-gift admission may still look like an author-coded exception and identify the culprit before Act III.
- Added guilty speech length may become a tell. Measurement alone cannot settle this; readers must judge the spoken pattern.
- A fragment can establish source contact without conclusively establishing a pour. The final lead must follow the combined case, with reasonable doubt about several alternatives.
- Ordinary favor handling must not become a repeated bottle-handling parade.
- The security failure needs to read as a pressured character's mistake, not a conveniently absent safety procedure.
- The bridge between paper disputes and real poison must not be an identical miniature legal defense in every character's voice.
- The new photo designs are requirements, not completed assets. The diagnostic text must not be promoted with older images that contradict it.
