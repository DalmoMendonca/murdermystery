## Feedback and limits

Readers still notice documentary explanation in the unchanged speeches, and the culprit's final contradictory assertion remains conspicuous. They consistently identify plausible alternatives: Chip's band stock and access, Brie's direct glass handling, Barb's recording suppression, Anya's key, and Hugh's knowledge of the original contents. Clean flavoring does not exclude a separate poisoning action. The strongest case remains circumstantial, not an exclusive physical match.

The revised speeches improve the measured ending breadth in this sample. They do not establish that all writing repetition or every murderer route is solved. Future work should address the remaining explanatory speeches and believable reasons for guilty characters to give their misleading accounts, while keeping this playable release available.
