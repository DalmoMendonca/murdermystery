# Quick full-cast diagnostic

The reader receives only the eight numbered checkpoint files, reads them sequentially and saves all thirty scores before opening the next checkpoint. Hidden selection and complete source are excluded. No Coming Clean, branch labels, route-check mappings or author arguments are shown.

The hidden culprit is reused from the earlier fifteen-role diagnostics rather than rerolled. This trial adds the remaining roles, all sixteen hunt clues and the current staged evidence. It can reveal whether the current case narrows plausibly, but does not isolate which change caused a difference from previous scores. One independent reader per condition adds reader variation. Intended image observations substitute for unfinished artwork.

Targets established before scoring: at least ten of thirty suspects strictly above five after Act II; culprit not practically obvious before Act III; correct sole final leader; three or four other suspects at five or six. Naturalness is a separate requirement, not inferred from scores. A numerical pass does not certify the other twenty-nine culprit selections, confirmed attendance, printed layouts or party pacing.

Frozen source and checkpoint hashes are recorded in the private manifest. The reporting script validates them and all 240 scores before calculating results. Earlier checkpoints must not be revised after later evidence is read.
