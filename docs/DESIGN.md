# Visual direction

## Approved homepage: After Hours

Dalmo selected After Hours on October 4, 2026 and requested a simple landing page that fits one viewport on phones and desktops. The approved world is a dark museum after closing: marble lion, burgundy velvet, warm ivory type, gold utility text, and a red primary download action. This explicit choice supersedes the earlier teal homepage baseline below. Keep only the museum identity, title, short description, date/location/player count, both downloads, repository link, and spoiler notice. Preserve the theatrical art; remove extra promotional copy and captions. Size and position the content responsively rather than hiding overflowing content. Allow normal document reflow for accessibility zoom.

Use Dalmo's existing sites as the design baseline rather than generic AI-generated landing-page conventions.

## Default principles

- Favor a strong single composition over stacked marketing sections.
- Minimal copy. No explanatory microcopy unless it earns its space.
- No generic SaaS card grids, stat cards, pills, glass panels, or gradient-heavy hero templates.
- Avoid faux-luxury/editorial templates (beige paper, gold rules, giant elegant serif + eyebrow labels) unless explicitly requested.
- Prefer flat fields of color, confident typography, asymmetry, whitespace, and playful but deliberate interaction.
- Mobile-first and safe-area aware.
- No scrolling when the content comfortably fits on one screen.
- For this project, use the dalmo.ai family resemblance: teal #057294, cream #fff3d6, Georgia/serif display typography, system sans-serif utility copy, minimal chrome.
- Do not use the copy pattern “X is Y. Not Z.”

The project homepage should remain utility-first: identify the project and expose the two downloads. It is not a marketing site.
