# Faye watermark experiment — rejected

One image was generated with the built-in image tool, copied to `press-preview-v1.png`, and inspected both directly and in `preview.html` at full, ordinary publicity-crop, and 160-pixel thumbnail sizes. The crop uses the same photograph through CSS. No watermark was moved and no alternate photograph was substituted.

The ordinary repeated watermark does **not** convincingly become a star engraved on the wine glass. The closest stars sit outside the bowl. At thumbnail size the image loses detail; that is not positive evidence for the intended misidentification. A photographer's confident physical-glass claim would require a conveniently relocated logo or a deliberately engineered crop.

**Discard the watermark-confusion premise. Do not regenerate until a logo happens to land in the needed spot.** It does not solve the guilty-answer style risk.

The publicity crop does naturally remove the display's ON LOAN credit while preserving the donor, bowl and drink. That part of the photographic scandal is usable draft material. The visible drinking glass is ordinary and unengraved. A separate proposed innocent payoff may compare that recorded ordinary glass with the identified gift, rather than pretend the watermark is physical. This is a new premise, not acceptance inherited from the failed experiment.

No player exhibit, PDF, download or production page has been updated. The prototype page was inspected in a temporary background browser tab; the same full/cropped views remain reproducible from the saved HTML and image.
