# Revised game flow

The previous release was mechanically incomplete. “Investigate and roleplay” did not ensure that any question was asked, any alibi reached the room or any essential evidence was shared. Murder narration beside an innocent narrative invited accidental disclosure. The 2025 reference kit supplies the useful structure: a timed statement, new evidence, explicit questions, safe branch answers and a turn for every guest.

## Act I: arrive, mingle, discover

Give guests their introduction, private background/tasks and hidden animal slip. Three printed social tasks provide a way to start talking; a missing named guest does not block a task. Scavenger clues add atmosphere and grievances but are not essential evidence. The donor remains fictional/offstage. Fictional times are not guests' actual movements.

## Act II: two hearings

At death, draw from attending core animals without seeing which guest has the animal. Optional animals are disjoint. Each player keeps their slip and quietly selects the called/not-called script. F1–F2 establish poison and the time window. In Hearing 1 everyone reads a short motive answer. F3–F4 then establish missing toxin and camera gap. In Hearing 2 everyone gives a prepared whereabouts account.

Seats follow ascending attending IDs. The previous occupied seat asks the shared question; the respondent answers, then asks the next occupied seat. The facilitator ticks each ID. Skip absent IDs. A neighbour may read for someone who prefers help; the guest indicates just their selected speech box. No invented alibis or facts. Acting is optional.

## Act III: evidence hearing, discussion, ballot

F5 completes the fixed reports. Guests open their sealed EVIDENCE envelope, select their assigned A/B letter privately and keep the unused card hidden. Count one card per attending ID before continuing. Everyone reads their selected card, then the selected scripted reply; the card goes on the Evidence Table. All evidence reaches everyone without relying on assertive guests or a crowded table.

Pair discussion explicitly asks for suspect, motive, claimed time/location and confirming/contradicting card. Up to three brief challenges can repeat the prepared answers. Everyone votes privately. Collect all ballots and prevent changes before finale envelopes open.

## Finale

The called animal identifies themselves only after voting. That guest opens their separate named FINALE envelope and reads the actual confession. Other finale envelopes stay closed. Do not solicit ranked innocent/guilty confessions before the reveal. Ties at third place include every positive-vote name at the cutoff; zero-vote guests do not count. Tally the ranking, reveal, then award.

## Logical proof and limits

Every innocent has continuous personally witnessed presence elsewhere, not merely activity on an unattended fax or scanner. Optional alibis use fixed staff independently of other guests. The selected murderer claims elsewhere presence but has an authenticated timed incident record at the private glass; all other guests have continuous alibis. The real murder narrative stays in spoiler-only canon and the sealed confession, outside playable pages.

This makes the mystery accessible and more straightforward than the earlier ambiguous traces. Forty-five murderer/attendance combinations are simulated; every one produces exactly one conflicting record. These are data/content tests and an independent behavior review, not a human playtest or measured performance guarantee. A rehearsal with casual readers remains the right way to assess fun and pacing.
