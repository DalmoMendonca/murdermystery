# Pass 15 — SPOILERS

This round tests an architectural change, not revised adjectives. The original toxic artifact stays in locked storage; its empty replica goes to the exhibit trolley. A criminal forces the cabinet. The theft needs no authorized poisonous preview, distracted custodian, front-only inspection, spare key, or synchronized alibi. The shared reception sideboard remains ordinary service rather than a second failure of hazardous-object custody. Historical toxic objects are plausible collection hazards; this fictional loan and theft are invented, not claims about a real museum's practices.

Opportunity describes each person's own consequential scandal. Its innocent and guilty versions are deliberately identical in this diagnostic round. Nobody alone volunteers preview–packing–target access in that release. Method then asks about the objects and actions previously introduced; its guilty account admits an additional small bottle at the target, while innocent accounts resolve the earlier suspicion through actual documents, objects, and consequences. The transport bottle is unmarked; residue is compatible, not unique identification. Coming Clean states theft, delivery and motive already supported before voting.

This makes the early branches neutral but risks moving the entire murderer signal to one conspicuous late admission. It also leaves many innocent explanations as comparative evidence rather than full exclusion. Those are explicit test questions, not problems hidden by scoring. Do not publish this version just because a reader selects the guilty small-bottle speaker. It must feel like a story and meet breadth and final-rival goals, not merely pass a label-recognition exercise.

Thirty individually authored Opportunity and sixty Method answers are in `rounds.yaml`; existing public copy is locked. The Method carrier detail recurs across alternative guilty worlds because the crime uses a common delivery route. That does not excuse repetitive style or weak character-specific causality; reviewers must assess both. The packet questions are provisional and require reconciliation with this changed evidence before integration.

The installed Game Narrative and Genre Craft skills guide the two-story structure, visible plants, delayed meaning and resolved red herrings. Story Project's causal scene discipline is applied to the existing kit; no novel project or new Story CLI build replaces the repository pipeline. All failed trials remain archived. These are AI transcript tests, not estimates of human difficulty.
