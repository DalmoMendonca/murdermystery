## Limits

This is a complete rewrite and consistency/visual review, not a new blind player trial. Earlier 15/15 and 14/14 solve results describe rejected or superseded speeches. There is no new solve percentage or measured difficulty rating. The requested structure intentionally allows a careful listener with complete evidence to clear every innocent; logical solvability does not guarantee satisfying human difficulty. Identity is supplied by the scripted account matched to anonymous certified sources, not independent facial identification. Physical print quality, live listening burden and actual party pacing remain unmeasured.
