## What improved

The mounting failure, interrupted reception, unpaid course, and unattended glass form an understandable causal sequence. Reviewers found the distinction between canceled performance, lighting, and camera systems useful. Keeping poison in the tampered bottle avoids an obvious empty-container alarm being ignored. Ordinary professional disputes feel more credible than an evening of synchronized alibis or repeated bottle handling.

All three final rankings had an evidence basis: source-access walkthrough, physical material recorded among belongings, and actual donor-glass encounter. Reviewers did not rely solely on a speech sounding guilty. The innocent scandals also retained some residual suspicion rather than all dropping to 1.

## What still fails

1. **Convergence is too convenient.** Twenty-two or thirty people appear to confront Grant around one place setting during one brief delay. Replacing repetitive errands with a common confrontation created a different version of the same engineering problem.
2. **The speaking structure gives clues about authorship.** Innocents repeatedly admit misconduct and cite documents explaining it. The selected guilty Method account is shorter and less exculpatory. That is an avoidable signal even when evidence also supports the ranking.
3. **Method introduces too much essential behavior.** The three selected culprits disclose direct glass movement late. Earlier accounts establish their disputes and presence, but the final release supplies part of the decisive act rather than solely reinterpreting an established detail.
4. **Act II narrows by explicit glass contact.** Brie, Al, and Dada become recurring leaders. Increasing everybody's glass contact would recreate the repetition the user rejected. The evidence-release architecture needs changing, not more manufactured touches.
5. **Material similarity remains circumstantial.** Common foil stock, no torn-edge fit, and no poison finding on the selected carrier leave real alternatives. A folder or clipboard in two places does not establish how poison traveled. Do not turn clearance metadata into a claim of conclusive evidence.
6. **Explaining a scandal is not excluding a murder.** Four late admissions provide real source-window exclusions; many other innocent accounts merely explain professional misconduct. This does not satisfy the stronger all-innocent-exclusion architecture yet.
7. **The poison acquisition remains a convenient premise.** Donor insistence explains the hazardous original but does not make the setup ordinary. Reviewers accept it as a party convention while still identifying it as contrived.

## Next design changes, before another rewrite

- Keep the three conflicts, but spread them over the actual exhibition preparations and reception. Only a small number of shared incidents should occur at Grant's table. Give each incident one reason to happen, witnesses, consequences, and a later surviving record; stop scripting one confrontation per guest.
- Redesign evidence timing. Act II should leave multiple plausible delivery routes unresolved; Act III can establish which object was contaminated. Do not make the final answer depend on learning the culprit's glass contact for the first time in Method. Plot physical encounters before assigning innocent/guilty text.
- Build a concrete case for each culprit substitution using those incidents, then check that each innocent alternative is genuinely incompatible with the crime or remains an intentional plausible rival. Clearly distinguish a best-supported inference from a uniquely proved solution.
- Rewrite innocent and guilty speeches with comparable narrative shape and character-specific voice. Innocents can resist, joke, blame, qualify, or admit only the relevant part; not everyone gives a miniature documentary defense. Guilty text should reinterpret earlier actions without standing out by length.
- Settle the trace's physical meaning at the story level before commissioning art. A merely compatible scrap is not a definitive link, and adding a unique match too early would over-solve the game. Avoid practical poison preparation details.
- Audit all thirty culprit paths, endings, questions, and twenty-two-person dependencies. Coming Clean and some Method text in this prototype retain previous-draft wording and are not publication-ready.
