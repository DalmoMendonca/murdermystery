# Round 21 — competing decisions, not competing table visits

Status: private authoring; not frozen, tested, integrated or accepted.

Round20's four readers found the intended physical case, but the competing guests mostly had motive plus paper access. This pass changes the investigation story. A gala contains several conflicts happening at once; its guests should be pursuing those conflicts, not each taking a numbered turn at the reception sideboard.

## Five intersecting scenes

1. **The gift negotiation.** Artie tries to prevent an announcement; Monet uses service payment as leverage. Their interference leaves a personal toast proceeding without an attendant. Their actions concern different kinds of authority, not duplicate requests to correct a speech.
2. **The failed installation.** Chip protects his treatment record; Barb suppresses a warning; Frank retrieves equipment used without approval. These are separately motivated safety failures. No one gets a perfect time certificate. Tools, access and withheld knowledge are consequential, but negligence is not automatically poison administration.
3. **The performance dispute.** Dada deliberately stages an apparent collapse with a rehearsal glass and jacket; Minnie tries to erase a rejected blackout. Their actions become alarming when Grant really collapses. The canceled show and the separate service glass resolve the planned-effect hypothesis in Act III.
4. **The private confrontation.** Brie stops service to demand payment; Al obstructs reception with disputed sculpture equipment; Penny conceals a family letter in a small case; Paige retrieves her recorder from Grant's coat. Direct target access and portable containers give these rivals a different case from source-handling staff.
5. **The historical inquiry.** Art and Reed conceal failures in their research. Their interest in the historical poison is genuine and professionally compromising. The original/copy distinction reinterprets what they requested; do not invent chemical expertise or secret laboratory access for them.

Other guests continue their disputes in the gallery, office, loading area and dining room. Some still use reception because it genuinely has programs and service; it must stop functioning as everyone's compulsory evidence station. Presence elsewhere is a scene, not a continuous alibi.

## Release discipline

Act I supplies stakes and unresolved actions. Act II reveals the most concerning decisions and their consequences. Where an innocent explanation materially weakens a suspect, put its supporting context in Act III, not in the same sentence as the incriminating admission. That context must have an earlier plant. Coming Clean pays off secrets; it must not introduce an essential exclusion for the first time.

The original/copy comparison remains a delayed *interpretation* of a visible object. Nothing in this pass may hide visible neck damage or turn endpoint photographs into surveillance. The final selected case remains a conjunction of motive, source conduct and target conduct. Anonymous carrier evidence is a known attribution weakness; evaluate whether the planted conjunction is persuasive before adding a new individual mark or an implausible universal favor inventory.
