Introductions / 22 attending characters

Artie Ficial / Executive Director of the Meridian Museum
I'm Artie Ficial. It is my pleasure to welcome you to our gala unveiling our newest exhibition, Treasures of the World, sponsored by our generous benefactor, Grant Larceny, who will arrive here shortly. I'm confident this will be a remarkable evening, and if anything goes wrong tonight, please treat it as an immersive experience.

Claire O’Scuro / Chief Curator of World Cultures
I’m Claire O’Scuro. I am delighted to meet anyone who knows the difference between provenance and a price tag.

Hugh Bidder / International Art Dealer & Collector Liaison
I’m Hugh Bidder. Everything has a price. My charming conversation is the only exception, and even that is negotiable.

Monet Bags / Chief Financial Officer of the Meridian Museum
I’m Monet Bags. I'm the CFO and I oversee the numbers. Please enjoy the gala, but know that any additional expenditures will not be reimbursed without a receipt.

Chip Patina / Chief Conservator
I’m Chip Patina. Please admire the art without touching it. That includes touching it to demonstrate that you are not touching it.

Dada DiCapo / Performance Artist & Cultural Celebrity
I’m Dada DiCapo. If the room feels uncomfortable, good. I may invoice you for the feeling. Art is money. Art is life.

Vincent Van Faux / Celebrated Contemporary Painter
I’m Vincent Van Faux. My new work is here tonight. My early work is also here, despite several very expensive objections.

Sue Venir / Cultural Property Attorney
I’m Sue Venir. Lovely to meet you. Nothing I say over dinner constitutes legal advice, although I will be keeping notes.

Dr. Art E. Fact / Professor of Art History & Provenance Scholar
I’m Dr. Art E. Fact. I bring decades of scholarship and a footnote for almost every possible dinner conversation. Looking forward to intellectual discussions tonight!

Tess Tament / Philanthropist & Heir to the Larceny Family Fortune
I’m Tess Tament. I represent the family foundation. Contrary to what some here may think, being related to a donor is a responsibility, not a cash machine.

Barb Dwyer / Director of Museum Security & Former Art-Crimes Investigator
I’m Barb Dwyer. I am security. Please behave as though I have already noticed whatever you are about to try.

Paige Turner / Investigative Arts Journalist
I’m Paige Turner. I am covering the gala. I just want to say in advance that absolutely nothing is off the record tonight.

Frank Lloyd Wrong / Exhibition Architect & Designer
I'm Frank Lloyd Wrong. I designed all the displays you see around the gallery. If one more person says they have one tiny last-minute change, I'm gonna scream!

Mona Lott / International Cultural Heritage Ambassador
I’m Mona Lott. I negotiate the return of precious objects. I expect several difficult conversations and at least one excellent croissant.

Elon Mosaic / Tech Founder, Collector & Museum Patron
I’m Elon Mosaic. I collect art. Some people collect grudges about the art I collect. Both are expensive hobbies.

Elle Loominate / Textile Artist & Designer
I’m Elle Loominate. I make installations with light and fabric. Please do not describe either as something your nephew could do.

Reed DeLabel / Doctoral Fellow in Art History
I’m Reed DeLabel. I am the graduate fellow. I know where the footnotes go and, more dangerously, where they are missing.

Al Baster / Sculptor & Public-Art Star
I’m Al Baster. I made the entrance sculpture. I would appreciate your admiration in a tone that suggests prompt payment.

Brie DeVivre / Head Caterer & Hospitality Director

Anne E. Dote / Director of Education & Docent Programs

Penny Pincher / Eccentric Private Collector

Justin Tyme / Independent Blogger & Local Truthstorian
