## Scandal explanations versus murder exclusions

Several printed clues are fairly resolved:

These explanations remove specific proposed mechanisms or misleading object identities. They do not automatically clear the people. Clear sauce excludes poisoning that batch, not Brie touching an empty donor glass by another means. Unused stock in a kit does not prove its owner never obtained another piece of the same common stock. A clean prop excludes that prop as the vehicle, not Dada as a person. The packet generally acknowledges the material limits, which is good; the final speeches sometimes encourage stronger acquittals than their actual evidence warrants.

## Actual ambiguities

1. Who was present through the preview, and who had unobserved access while West left? Art is established, Chip is closely involved, but a complete attendee roll is not released.
2. Who could reach the sideboard during 6:32–6:44? Several admitted contacts are identified, but broad guest traffic leaves others possible.
3. Can Art's packing be independently tied to the poisoned vial or glass? The laboratory explicitly says no unique tear match.
4. When and by whom was the vial discarded? A reception-bin location is compatible with several guests.
5. Is Art's correction really received before the gala? His final claim is self-report; if corroborated, it weakens one apparent concealment motive without removing his grievance over coerced authority.
6. Could Chip or Minnie obtain both source and delivery access? No positive full-route evidence is supplied, but their exclusion is incomplete.

Accomplices, an unseen second poison source, laboratory falsification or a secret later cupboard opening are invented alternatives, not supported deductions. They should not be inflated into equal theories. The ordinary alternatives above arise from expressly open access and common stock.

## Contrivances and concrete repairs

- Give West a work-specific reason to leave and establish it early: the revised receipt discrepancy can appear in the preview instruction, with a named escort or contemporaneous note. Preserve the hidden split, but avoid making every precaution fail without social cause.
- Show a short preview attendance record and a clearly bounded sideboard observation record. If the intended solution needs true uniqueness, add independent coverage that excludes remaining source-plus-delivery combinations; do not imply that explaining a scandal is an alibi.
- Keep the nonunique laboratory conclusion. Add one independently observed action or item linking the suspect's carried paper to the vial, rather than converting common trim into a magical unique material.
- Clarify the transfer physics: establish how the paper is used around the sample container and why a fragment could enter the glass. Do not require guests to invent a foil funnel after the fact.
- Plant Art's carried packing naturally through another witness seeing the cards bundled at the preview, instead of having him volunteer the future trace. Let his own speech focus on professional injury and disagreement.
- Replace some full confessional monologues with evasions, interruptions, anger, misplaced pride or short factual answers. Keep necessary facts on readable objects or independent witness cards. Different characters should not all deliver the same mature account of their own complicity.
- Provide the actual dated correction receipt if it matters. Otherwise the last claim sounds like an unresolved new defense, and the audience cannot decide how much motive survives.
- Make the 6:35 hold an initial mounting concern and 6:37 the confirmed crack, or align the records.
- Reduce the thirty separate scandal arcs' rhetorical repetition. Preserve parallel motives, but let the physical investigation carry the final narrowing.

The party has a coherent central mechanism and meaningful fair planting. Its strongest improvement would be firmer independent opportunity evidence and less uniformly forensic self-explanation in the guest speeches.
