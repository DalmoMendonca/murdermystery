Question for Artie Ficial, Monet Bags, Sue Venir, Tess Tament: What would Grant's terms change for the museum or your family?

Artie Ficial
Please, sit wherever you can hear. Grant wanted his name over the door, a vote in every department, and my resignation. Monet needed his check for wages; Sue objected to the terms. Six years here, and tonight I was supposed to introduce my own replacement. My father would have called that a useful lesson in ambition. I had hoped never to give him the satisfaction.

Monet Bags
Friday. That's when wages are due. My mother used to wait by the phone on payday. I know what that does to a household. Grant's check could cover our staff; his terms let him question every transfer. Artie hated losing authority. I needed an amount I could put through payroll. Everyone kept saying rescue. Nobody gave me a date for the money.

Sue Venir
He intended to announce the gift before a board vote. Its terms gave him approval of future acquisitions. I had advised on the troublesome ownership file already. Mona and I can argue a case all afternoon and still have dinner together. Grant thought disagreement meant somebody must lose. I wanted his microphone stopped until there was an agreement worth announcing.

Tess Tament
Family gift for the photograph. His gift when I asked for a say. I asked Sue about the succession clause before agreeing to pose. Grant wanted me glamorous and agreeable beside it. I know how to do both. Hugh has seen me fail at agreeable. I wanted a chair in the decisions, and I wanted Grant to think I deserved it. Those wants kept getting mixed up.

The bowl's ownership papers were incomplete. I approved its label too soon; Grant preferred that earlier version. Reed kept asking me to revisit it. I taught that student to question an authority, and then behaved very badly when the authority was me. Grant wanted my name beneath the claim tonight. I wanted the revised label up. We were each rather determined.

Chip Patina
Recent repair. Grant wanted me to call it ancient. He liked my name beneath his description. We had already argued over treatment paperwork. Then the entrance mount started moving. People sighed when I stopped the conversation. They always do. I am easier company if nobody touches anything. Claire knows that. Tonight I was exceptionally poor company.

Dr. Art E. Fact
Reed found a gap in my ownership citation. Grant found it inconvenient, which is not a scholarly objection. I asked to hold the earlier press copies; he wanted my name on them before the correction. Dada was here. I had hoped to discuss something rather more impressive with her. A ridiculous concern, given the size of the actual error. Still, there it is.

Reed DeLabel

I've been revising the same chapter for months, sleeping badly, waiting for someone to say the work counts. Claire asked for another draft. Grant called it a student's publicity stunt. I wasn't particularly gracious about either.

Hugh Bidder
Grant wanted a refund on the Star Bowl and a public admission that its certificate was false. My firm supplied it. I changed our file instead of admitting we'd been fooled. Elon began worrying about his other purchases; the auctioneer was available to help him worry. I offered a settlement. Grant preferred an audience. Some of you knew me before I had a respectable office. He liked reminding me.

Vincent Van Faux
He kept exhibiting the early picture. My breakthrough, apparently. I painted it to pay rent. Now he'd commissioned the bottle study and wanted to print it beside his rescue announcement. I withdrew permission. Hugh told me to be less difficult. Dada, you kept making new work while I got invited to explain the old one. Grant liked having that old version of me on his wall.

Elon Mosaic
Grant wanted the platform, the collection, the customer list. I'd pitched the demo harder than the installation justified. He could cut funding and make that difference public. I asked to leave Hugh's disputed purchase out of the collector photograph. I wanted one launch that belonged to me. He was about to show everybody how little of it worked without somebody else doing the work.

Penny Pincher
I bought the family shares using a damaged example as the comparison. Our piece was intact. Then I spent much more with a dealer. Grant had both figures for the handout. I removed it. I manage the family things because someone has to, you know? That used to be what they said about me. I wanted to speak to them before Grant did.
