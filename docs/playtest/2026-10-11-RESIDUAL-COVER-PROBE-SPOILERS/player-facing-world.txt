RELEASE 1
Exactly one of these 22 characters committed the murder alone. Other mentioned staff are not selectable suspects.

Artie Ficial / Executive Director of the Meridian Museum
I'm Artie Ficial. It is my pleasure to welcome you to our gala unveiling our newest exhibition, Treasures of the World, sponsored by our generous benefactor, Grant Larceny, who will arrive here shortly. I'm confident this will be a remarkable evening, and if anything goes wrong tonight, please treat it as an immersive experience.

Claire O’Scuro / Chief Curator of World Cultures
I’m Claire O’Scuro. I am delighted to meet anyone who knows the difference between provenance and a price tag.

Hugh Bidder / International Art Dealer & Collector Liaison
I’m Hugh Bidder. Everything has a price. My charming conversation is the only exception, and even that is negotiable.

Monet Bags / Chief Financial Officer of the Meridian Museum
I’m Monet Bags. I'm the CFO and I oversee the numbers. Please enjoy the gala, but know that any additional expenditures will not be reimbursed without a receipt.

Chip Patina / Chief Conservator
I’m Chip Patina. Please admire the art without touching it. That includes touching it to demonstrate that you are not touching it.

Dada DiCapo / Performance Artist & Cultural Celebrity
I’m Dada DiCapo. If the room feels uncomfortable, good. I may invoice you for the feeling. Art is money. Art is life.

Vincent Van Faux / Celebrated Contemporary Painter
I’m Vincent Van Faux. My new work is here tonight. My early work is also here, despite several very expensive objections.

Sue Venir / Cultural Property Attorney
I’m Sue Venir. Lovely to meet you. Nothing I say over dinner constitutes legal advice, although I will be keeping notes.

Dr. Art E. Fact / Professor of Art History & Provenance Scholar
I’m Dr. Art E. Fact. I bring decades of scholarship and a footnote for almost every possible dinner conversation. Looking forward to intellectual discussions tonight!

Tess Tament / Philanthropist & Heir to the Larceny Family Fortune
I’m Tess Tament. I represent the family foundation. Contrary to what some here may think, being related to a donor is a responsibility, not a cash machine.

Barb Dwyer / Director of Museum Security & Former Art-Crimes Investigator
I’m Barb Dwyer. I am security. Please behave as though I have already noticed whatever you are about to try.

Paige Turner / Investigative Arts Journalist
I’m Paige Turner. I am covering the gala. I just want to say in advance that absolutely nothing is off the record tonight.

Frank Lloyd Wrong / Exhibition Architect & Designer
I'm Frank Lloyd Wrong. I designed all the displays you see around the gallery. If one more person says they have one tiny last-minute change, I'm gonna scream!

Mona Lott / International Cultural Heritage Ambassador
I’m Mona Lott. I negotiate the return of precious objects. I expect several difficult conversations and at least one excellent croissant.

Elon Mosaic / Tech Founder, Collector & Museum Patron
I’m Elon Mosaic. I collect art. Some people collect grudges about the art I collect. Both are expensive hobbies.

Elle Loominate / Textile Artist & Designer
I’m Elle Loominate. I make installations with light and fabric. Please do not describe either as something your nephew could do.

Reed DeLabel / Doctoral Fellow in Art History
I’m Reed DeLabel. I am the graduate fellow. I know where the footnotes go and, more dangerously, where they are missing.

Al Baster / Sculptor & Public-Art Star
I’m Al Baster. I made the entrance sculpture. I would appreciate your admiration in a tone that suggests prompt payment.

Brie DeVivre / Head Caterer & Hospitality Director
I’m Brie DeVivre. I am responsible for dinner. If you enjoy it, remember my name. If you do not, blame the donor’s instructions.

Anne E. Dote / Director of Education & Docent Programs
I’m Anne E. Dote. I run education here. I can make almost anything interesting, including a room full of adults behaving badly.

Penny Pincher / Eccentric Private Collector
I’m Penny Pincher. Some things in this museum belonged to my family, so you'd think I'd at least get a discount on tickets. I'm delighted about all the free food tonight, though!

Justin Tyme / Independent Blogger & Local Truthstorian
I'm Justin Tyme. I run a local history blog. If your family funded this wing, I'd love to hear about it. I brought the property records.

Question for Artie Ficial, Monet Bags, Sue Venir, Tess Tament: What would Grant's terms change for the museum or your family?

Artie Ficial
Monet, will that check clear before Friday? I still don't have an answer. Grant wanted his name over the door, a vote in every department, and my resignation. Sue objected to the terms. Six years here. I had written the introduction for the man replacing me. It's in my pocket. I keep reaching for it.

Monet Bags
Friday. That's when wages are due. My mother used to wait by the phone on payday. I know what that does to a household. Grant's check could cover our staff; his terms let him question every transfer. Artie hated losing authority. I needed an amount I could put through payroll. Everyone kept saying rescue. Nobody gave me a date for the money.

Sue Venir
He intended to announce the gift before a board vote. Its terms gave him approval of future acquisitions. I had advised on the troublesome ownership file already. Mona and I can argue a case all afternoon and still have dinner together. Grant thought disagreement meant somebody must lose. I wanted his microphone stopped until there was an agreement worth announcing.

Tess Tament
Family gift for the photograph. His gift when I asked for a say. I asked Sue about the succession clause before agreeing to pose. Grant wanted me glamorous and agreeable beside it. I know how to do both. Hugh has seen me fail at agreeable. I wanted a chair in the decisions, and I wanted Grant to think I deserved it. Those wants kept getting mixed up.

Question for Claire O’Scuro, Chip Patina, Dr. Art E. Fact, Reed DeLabel: What problem in the collection work could Grant use against you?

Claire O’Scuro
The bowl's ownership papers were incomplete. I approved its label too soon; Grant preferred that earlier version. Reed kept asking me to revisit it. I taught that student to question an authority, and then behaved very badly when the authority was me. Grant wanted my name beneath the claim tonight. I wanted the revised label up. We were each rather determined.

Chip Patina
Recent repair. Not ancient. Grant wanted ancient on the label, with my name beneath it. We'd already argued over treatment paperwork. Then the entrance mount moved. I stopped the conversation. Claire, you know me: I'm bad company when things are coming loose. I was bad company tonight.

Dr. Art E. Fact
Reed found a gap in my ownership citation. Grant found it inconvenient, which is not a scholarly objection. I asked to hold the earlier press copies; he wanted my name on them before the correction. Dada was here. I had hoped to discuss something rather more impressive with her. A ridiculous concern, given the size of the actual error. Still, there it is.

Reed DeLabel
I found the missing document. I changed the bowl entry. Grant wanted settled ownership put back, and I wanted my name on the correction. Yes, my name. Claire, it's the same chapter. Again. Question for Hugh Bidder, Vincent Van Faux, Elon Mosaic, Penny Pincher: What dispute did your dealings with Grant leave you hiding?

Hugh Bidder

Vincent Van Faux

I withdrew permission. Hugh told me to be less difficult. Dada, you kept making new work while I got invited to explain the old one. Grant liked having that old version of me on his wall.

Elon Mosaic
Grant wanted the platform, the collection, the customer list. All in one deal. Monet asked what it cost. Hugh's disputed purchase was going into the collector photograph, so I asked to leave it out. I wanted to launch something that was mine. Hugh keeps finding my discoveries for me. I know that's a ridiculous sentence. It is also more or less my life.

Penny Pincher
Please don't pass the family handout around. I bought their shares, then spent much more with a dealer. Grant had both figures. He wanted a comparison printed as though two purchases told him everything about a family. I removed it. They let me manage these things. I wanted to speak to them before he did. I haven't called.

Question for Frank Lloyd Wrong, Elle Loominate, Al Baster, Anne E. Dote: What would Grant change about your commission or public program?

Frank Lloyd Wrong

Anne E. Dote
I'd promised the school places before the grant was approved. Grant wanted their tour replaced by a patron preview. I put the school program back in the handout. He asked who authorized it. Fair question. I've stood in front of children before and told them a visit was canceled. I wasn't doing it again while a donor explained how generous he was being.
