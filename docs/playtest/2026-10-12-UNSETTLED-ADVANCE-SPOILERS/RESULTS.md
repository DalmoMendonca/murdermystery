Finale: The confession follows the pre-vote route closely: the physical bottle, stitch-only slides, and unattended glass already exposed the projection claim as a meaningful lie. The last line about never touching the fabric again is emotionally effective. The opening is somewhat forensic, reciting image evidence directly, but the personal ending earns its place. - moderate / naturalness: The final answers precisely resolve technical alternatives such as projection, camera installation, and stopper-band construction. These useful secondary lies compete for attention at the last investigative checkpoint. Suggested fix: Visually group secondary financial corrections in one compact document, retaining their explicit contradictions and avoiding additional explanatory speeches.
