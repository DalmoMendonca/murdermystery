## Cary Crates / t001

Accusation: Cary Crates

Cary claims the engraved glass stayed boxed, that service used a plain spare, and that investigators tested the unused gift. The manager specifically records unwrapping and setting the shop glass at 6:32, and the prepared-place and consumed-toast photographs show that same bare crest glass reaching Grant's lips. Cary is therefore trying to invalidate the actual poisoned vessel with a demonstrably false account. He delivered the gifts and later returned through service, providing access beyond the initial carton handover. Ordinary keys opened the bottle cupboard, and staff had the full contents warning. His concealed frame damage exposed him to an employer and insurer threat. Brie is the strongest alternative because she admits touching the glass alone, but her requested flavoring is confirmed and her retained container is cyanide-free. Her account fits the physical tests; Cary's account disputes their applicability through a false glass substitution. That particular lie makes him the best-supported accusation.

The mystery is solvable before voting through Cary's false plain-glass account, contradicted by independently retained service evidence and photographs. I appreciated that no unique torn-edge match was invented and that a correct drink order did not automatically clear Brie. The cast carries varied pressures: payroll, family care, withheld credit, professional shame, and restitution feel more substantial than interchangeable greed. Sue comforting Mona and Robin refusing to call death an answer were particularly effective. Voices differ in rhythm and concerns, although repeated admissions followed by a direct appeal to another attendee become conspicuous across so many speakers. The sequence feels deliberately assembled when the last round supplies one clean factual falsehood among extensive clarifications. Most of the solution comes from that final inconsistency rather than cumulative narrowing, since ordinary keys and broad staff knowledge leave many physical possibilities. Offstage managers, technicians, and editors are adequately attributed documentary witnesses; none needs to be a selectable suspect. The camera and studio explanations are clear, although some dialogue sounds designed to retire theories for the players.

Post-vote: Cary's confession follows the decisive pre-vote contradiction exactly: the crest glass was used, and his spare-glass story tried to break that evidence chain. Stealing poison during the preview and adding it later supplies the particular sequence, but those are confirmations of an available route rather than new facts needed to identify him. The clarification that the packing bin concerned frame damage usefully preserves the distinction between his two concealments. His fear of losing work and the final recognition of a larger loss make the admission emotionally credible. Chip and Barb's consequences also feel earned, though all three speakers remain unusually articulate about what their mistakes mean. The finale resolves the case without substituting a surprise forensic discovery.

- moderate / pacing: The decisive plain-spare claim arrives only in the final round; earlier evidence leaves many equally feasible private-place visitors and broadly shared bottle access. Suggested action: Seed Cary's claim that service never unpacked the gift in his second answer, while retaining the later photographic verification.

- minor / naturalness: Final statements explicitly close technical alternatives: proposed connection never installed, no live stunt, and no recording to recover. Together they read as an organized theory-elimination pass. Suggested action: Let one or two of those conclusions rest in the retained documents and spend the corresponding speech on an immediate personal conflict.

## Robin Banks / t002

Accusation: Robin Banks

The untrimmed interview records the manager leaving Grant's glass and returning to the kitchen. Grant then expressly calls the location his private room, addresses Paige's interview, and refuses Robin's petition there. Robin's final account instead puts this same argument at the public podium. This is a direct, material contradiction that conceals proximity to the bare prepared glass before its collection at 7:04. Active cyanide came from an accessible physical loan bottle, and a similar treatment-band scrap reached the glass; neither a projected object nor contamination of the shared punch explains the findings. Robin had visited intake and was determined to force Grant to hear the claimants. Brie is the strongest alternative because she actually prepared the individual drink alone, and a clean ingredient container cannot absolutely exclude deliberate addition. However, she acknowledges that opportunity, while Robin uniquely invents an incompatible location for a recorded encounter. The false location gives a positive discriminator beyond broad motive and access.

The final location contradiction is fair and satisfying to discover: the interview provides ordinary conversational words that independently test Robin's account. The glass, original contents, and physical bottle evidence establish a comprehensible method without requiring the confession. I would still distinguish a persuasive accusation from absolute exclusion of every alternative, because several people could poison the glass and the band scrap has no individual match. Brie's clean container alone does not exonerate her, which is a useful remaining uncertainty. The people acquire distinct emotional concerns: Monet's Friday payroll, Penny's family silence, Al's need to call home, and Artie's hunger for approval work particularly well. The repeated grouped questioning makes some speeches feel assembled to service exhibits, especially the last round's exact material and technical restatements. Offstage critic, photographer, dealer, and family references occasionally suggest additional participants before the opening rule resolves that they are not selectable. No deduction actually requires interviewing them. The range of consequences is stronger than a room full of identical career threats, although the sudden documented settlements arrive rather efficiently.

Post-vote: Robin's confession follows the decisive recorded-location contradiction and confirms the method already established by the poisoned glass and emptied active bottle. Taking poison during the preview and returning after the interview supplies the precise execution, which was inferential before voting; it does not replace the available reason to accuse Robin. The strongest emotional turn is recognizing that the claimants will now have to defend themselves against an unauthorized act, rather than treating murder as successful restitution. That consequence feels earned and distinct from the financial and professional embarrassments elsewhere. Brie's concern for staff transport also closes her thread naturally. Chip's final reflection is understandable, though slightly more aphoristic than his earlier practical speech.

- moderate / naturalness: Final speeches restate exhibit specifications almost verbatim: red face and silver back, resin and gold leaf, and power versus network. The repetition sounds designed to close deduction branches. Suggested action: Keep the exhibit facts and let one final response express a practical or emotional consequence rather than repeating all specifications.

- minor / evidence: Robin's false public-podium claim strongly identifies concealment, but poison transfer remains inferred while Brie independently admits unsupervised preparation. Clean flavoring does not rule out a separate deliberate addition. Suggested action: Add a small existing witness detail linking Robin's concealed private-room visit to handling the waiting glass, without naming the poison or supplying a full solution.

- minor / clarity: Camera-card and podcast evidence introduces photographer and critic functions that are not among the twenty-two named roles, producing momentary cast uncertainty. Suggested action: Label those exhibits as supplied by offstage agency staff when first introduced.
