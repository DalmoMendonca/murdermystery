How to read this audit

These are attentive AI text readers with access to all prior releases, not simulated human solve-rate estimates. Their recall and judgments can still contain errors. One reader per new-role scenario is a narrow diagnostic; the other20 selected-murderer scenarios were not tested this round. All 16 hunt clues are supplied to every reader; their physical discovery, table dynamics, acting, and visual interpretation are not tested. Readers have identical instructions and fresh context, rather than assigned personalities. Finale reviews use each reader’s top three suspects as a proxy for group voting.
