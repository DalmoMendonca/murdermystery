# Residual cover staged diagnostic

The candidate is an exact copy of the preceding residual-cover prototype. No story edits were made while testing. Three predetermined scenarios (Hugh Bidder, Penny Pincher, Elle O'Quent) are frozen independently for the 22- and 30-character casts. Hugh and Penny test whether their innocent scandal cover stories create unfair ambiguity when either becomes the murderer; Elle tests whether the prior final-only result survives sequential disclosure.

Each trial receives one fresh blind reader. All eight scores and the pre-finale accusation are immutable. No rerolls, no favorable-reader selection, and no expansion to a broad audit. The published fallback and sent public assets remain unchanged.

Promotion requires examining every completed diagnostic for earned deduction, timing, alternative suspects, and naturalness. The single final-only probe is not a controlled improvement claim. The current continuation starts only two readers; remaining cases stay unstarted until subsequent continuations.
