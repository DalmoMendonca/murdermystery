## What players reported

The obsolete proposal versus installation photograph made evidence comparison engaging. Innocent timing lies and invented early errands created competing suspects and delayed several early guesses. But a persistent transcript still allows a clean timing/apparatus overlap comparison. Readers repeatedly flagged identical secrecy endings and the selected guest's salon line as editorial giveaways. Innocent players needed clearer private instructions to preserve their false accounts. Hosts and older-guest personas requested full names, neutral rereads, a simple room plan and side-by-side dated images. Nobody's scripted hearing speech directly confessed or read an IF heading aloud; this does not test real guests making those mistakes.

## Changes made from this feedback

- Removed remaining generic Opportunity deflections and salon closings; thirty voices now use their own errands and concerns.
- Added thirteen innocent salon contacts, so salon mention alone does not identify the selected branch.
- Added private guidance that an innocent account can conceal another scandal; save corrections for Coming Clean.
- Made sixteen innocent Coming Clean speeches resolve their particular invented or shifted errand instead of repeating the same installation paragraph.
- Kept neutral discussion and rereads, full-name question targets, the attendance roster, dated proposal/actual photos and a room diagram. No clue-specific notes grid was restored.
- The old exact salon/key/linen classification was removed. Shared key and linen access do not name a person; reports identify the poisoned vessel and reconstruct the case.

## Scope and honest limits

An earlier three-reader trial of a single impossible alibi produced two correct innocent votes and difficulty2/5; it was rejected as too easy before this competing-liar revision. Raw case-A data remains preserved separately.

C02's stage1 write failed; its formulated earlier judgment was persisted only after stage2 opened. That exception is disclosed rather than described as perfect preregistration. Other stage feedback was saved before advancing according to agent reports. RESULTS.json records input and feedback hashes.

Final speech refinements followed these fixtures. No rerun with informed readers is presented as a fresh blind test. AI assessments did not test hunting, listening memory, negotiations, unprompted questions, printing, folding or actual timing. The final comparative solution remains discoverable with complete information; a human rehearsal is still needed to measure whether the party's mystery is too easy or too hard. No wrong guesses were manufactured to improve a statistic.
