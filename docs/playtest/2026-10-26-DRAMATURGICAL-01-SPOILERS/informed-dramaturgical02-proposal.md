# Informed dramaturgical consultation: proposed second pass

Status: editorial proposal only; no source changed. This consultant has read the solution-bearing candidate and cohort results and is ineligible for later blind reading. Candidate01, its complete cohort, the verified baseline, all evidence, and the public release remain separate preserved records.

## Finding

Candidate01 has distinct vocabulary and recognizable concerns, but still makes most people perform the same social act: present a professional defense, volunteer a private shame, and explain what the audience should learn from it. Moving a colleague's name to the front makes that defense addressed. It does not make a colleague able to resist it or change its purpose. Appending a practical request gives the speech an ending without changing the action through its middle.

The fresh cohort returned 17 correct accusations and 19 earned finales out of 22; naturalness averaged 6.05 and arc variety 6.95. Those are the recorded results, not estimates. The parent task supplies the verified baseline naturalness comparison of 6.41. The candidate has not demonstrated the desired improvement. The first table-read review correctly describes the remaining template, but its recommendation for a small polish should now be read alongside the completed adverse blind evidence.

Four wrong accusations choose innocent Elle: t009/Sue, t011/Barb, t010/Elon, and t021/Brie. The fifth selects Chip instead of Paige. Elle's route is printed; cloth carrying poison or concealment material is a reader inference. In t011 the reader explicitly uses her insistence on rolling alone as evidence of protecting an object from inspection. That extra implication is avoidable in prose. Her access and financial falsehood remain legitimate grounds for suspicion.

Other repeated complaints concern the same-sized testimony, convenient admissions, and the final documentary packet's comprehensive answers. The evidence packet and stages are frozen here. Many reader suggestions require added witnesses, shared errands, new times, earlier sampling disclosures, or new source documents. None is permissible in this pass. Preserve those complaints as limits; do not implement them under the name of dialogue polish.

## A feasible second-pass method

Work in a new versioned candidate. Keep candidate01's text, hashes, rendered packets, locked scores, and results intact. Preserve the verified baseline as the promotion fallback.

For every existing box, record its exact affirmative claims, denials, uncertainty, qualifiers, and knowledge at that stage. Record the claims as claims: an innocent's financial lie must remain false, and a murderer's technical lie must remain technically false. Timing, sequence, room, object, authorization, and whether an action happened belong in this ledger. Preserve the entry's original stage and selected branch. No factual claim moves to an earlier turn to make a scene smoother.

Then write a short private performance instruction: what is this speaker trying to make the room do, and what do they refuse to concede? Keep that instruction out of the player text. Write the entire box around that effort, attaching every required claim to the effort. A successful revision changes the relation between the claims: a subordinate detail offered to close a discussion, a technical specification used to give an order, a number used to overrule someone, an admission used to preserve a separate demand. Merely exchanging sentence order is insufficient.

Use contrast across the cast. Some turns may answer directly. Some may end on a stubborn claim. Some may concede an unflattering motive without agreeing to be comforted. Several should contain no named address, question, practical instruction, or final self-judgment. Uneven fluency is useful when it follows the person's predicament; arbitrary stammering and deliberately bad grammar are not useful.

All examples below replace wording inside one existing turn. They are sketches for semantic review, not approved manuscript patches. They require no reply, prop handling, offstage interview, player movement, or host decision. Quoted original wording is identified as such. Requests are character speech, not new game procedures.

## Six different dramatic through-lines

### 1. Elle: separate ownership from debt

**Action across the turns:** insist that her need for money does not entitle Grant to her pattern; withdraw the unsafe work despite the disappointing empty alcove; resist turning the payment argument into an argument about who owns her work; finally acknowledge a debt that still exists independently of ownership. The innocent can accept personal help after admitting the lie. The murderer loses the ability to bear touching the work. Those are distinct destinations already present in the candidate.

The useful conflict is autonomy versus dependence. Candidate01 inadvertently substitutes physical custody versus inspection. Innocent Method begins and ends by asserting exclusive control of the roll. Murderer Method also refuses help. Both therefore teach players to look inside the cloth. Remove that shared physical refusal. Keep the panel removal, lamp problem, private-room passage, rolling beside the set place, absence of Grant, departure with the panel, real bottle, slides, merchant dispute, and financial lie exactly where they already occur.

**Same-stage innocent Method shape:**

> The slides are enlarged stitches. The bottle beside the cloth was the real loan. I carried the panel through his room to pack it. The merchant charged the wrong job. I didn't use the other client's advance. Please leave the pattern out of the payment argument.

This preserves the explicit denials that evidence will contradict. It replaces both requests for exclusive physical handling with an attempt to control the terms of the argument. She can still be wrong and defensive. Nothing asserts that the cloth was clean, empty, continuously visible, incapable of concealing material, or inspected by Chip.

At innocent Motive, put the early-payment request and threatened pattern rights in active conflict instead of using Chip as an introductory relationship cue. At innocent Opportunity, let her refusal to reinstall remain the purpose of the box; include the complete route and financial denial as the account of what she did, without adding furtive gestures. At innocent Finale, retain the admission that she used the other advance, her continued debt, her denial of murder, the real preview bottle and passage past the glass, and the need to call the client whose work she has not made. Accepting help may remain, but need not be presented as if allowing someone near a suspicious object solves the mystery.

For murderer Method, retain projected bottle, physical loan in cupboard, and rights amendment as the actual false account. A self-contained rights claim can organize it: she is trying to retain the status of wronged artist. Do not make her more protective of the cloth to telegraph guilt. At Finale, preserve repaid advance versus earlier misuse and Grant's remaining power to expose it. Do not accidentally import innocent Elle's unpaid-debt state into this branch.

**Limit:** replacing the custody requests removes one unsupported behavioral cue. It cannot remove the physical possibility that the cloth carried something. A sentence saying it could not have done so would invent forensic evidence.

### 2. Barb: issue an operational instruction that eventually has nowhere to go

**Action across the turns:** authorize an exception; redefine it as manageable procedure; keep everyone occupied checking a recoverable record; lose that task when her own signature establishes there is nothing to recover. Innocent Barb stays accountable while resisting convenient stories about staff. Murderer Barb uses work as delay, then runs out of work she can give the room.

**Same-stage murderer Method shape:**

> Check local storage. The room check, including the glass, is recorded there. The technician removed the network lead, not the power. I used fault in the log to give Grant privacy. Wrong, yes. The footage still exists. Check it.

The factual claims and commands are already in this box. The revision makes the order the controlling action and removes the opening phrase that sounds like an answer to an unprinted accusation. Her confession of the false log is the concession she uses to make the false footage instruction credible. It does not become a balanced professional-and-emotional self-portrait.

The innocent Method must retain the opposite disconnection: power adapter removed, network lead attached, unpowered camera recorded nothing, false fault entry. Keep the recorder request only if it supports accepting a durable account of her decision. Do not imply a recording exists from the camera. Retain the existing statement about staff shifts without authorizing actual departures.

At murderer Finale, keep all source/poison/glass/privacy/false-network claims and the department-trust consequence. Move that consequence before the last return to unrecoverable footage. End the operational search. A new apology, future disciplinary outcome, or actual badge handover would give her an easier final job and weaken the specific collapse.

### 3. Monet: force acceptance of a result while refusing scrutiny of its means

**Action across the turns:** demand wages be treated as urgent; route the bill away from the museum; insist the paid wages settle the relevant argument; finally reveal that wages remain her reflex after they have ceased to excuse anything. Innocent Monet accepts the unauthorized transfer under her own name but remains worried about Friday. Murderer Monet's final repetition is involuntary, not a well-formed ethical conclusion.

**Same-stage innocent Method shape:**

> The staff got paid. Grant was to pay for his extra course. Service was to carry out the original toast order; it stayed booked. I had authority to transfer the wage money. A missing approval on the payroll line is an office problem. I'm not apologizing for paying people.

Every material proposition is already present. The speech starts with the outcome she wants accepted, disposes of the service distinction, and ends by refusing the moral premise of scrutiny. The claim to authority is still false; she has not partly confessed early. The final refusal makes the speech an attempt to close a matter, rather than another request for reassurance.

Keep murderer Method's 6:15 assertion, unwrapped-glass invoice, before-preview return, denial of returning, wrong-manager-time claim, and later-cheese-ticket separation. The deliberate precision is credible for this particular speaker and should survive. If every other character becomes equally exact, that contrast disappears. Do not remove the glass/time contradiction for naturalness.

At murderer Finale retain the separately returned ticket, later visit after the preview, actual poisoning, false time and reason the time fails, restricted funds and donor pressure, and the thought of Grant's family waiting. Put the family thought before the last involuntary return to wages if changing the current ending. Do not finish by explaining the psychology of the repetition for the audience.

### 4. Cary: keep the argument at the scale of a job he understands

**Action across the turns:** avoid discussing a report; seek something to move; dispute what a photograph can require him to sign; accept a specific repair charge in innocence or find that his preferred escape into useful labor is gone in guilt. His body may be part of performance, but no new lifting, touching, destroying, or disposing occurs in the room.

**Same-stage innocent Method shape:**

> I'm not signing another damage report because somebody prefers one picture. Different light. That's what's different in the receiving picture. The crack was there when I delivered the frame. Service unpacked the crest glass from my carton; you see that engraving in the drinking photograph.

The original facts and denial all remain. He begins with the boundary he is trying to enforce. The bottle/glass delivery clarification is then a work fact he can answer directly. He does not end by turning that fact into an analysis of his character. Keep its brevity; adding a partner's name or a final plea would restore the template.

The murderer must still admit loosening the stopper at the preview as a packing/condition check, claim inert contents, insist the engraved glass remained boxed and a plain spare was used, and distinguish correcting the frame report from the service claim. Do not substitute Mona's sealed-water defense or Brie's pitcher defense.

At innocent Finale, retain the crack, false old-damage report, discarded broken bit, false light excuse, both deliveries, murder denial, personal repair charge and future need to disclose. That is a concrete cost, not a declaration of redemption. At murderer Finale, place the loss comparison before the last existing line about nobody asking him to move anything. Preserve photographs, employer/unsafe-signature coercion, extraction and later poisoning, used crest glass and its documentary support, and the purpose of the spare-glass lie.

### 5. Dada: insist on the ugliness of the intended work, then refuse its artistic rescue

**Action across the turns:** demand authorship of a public insult; keep pursuing a rejected cue; refuse anyone's attempt to make the intended humiliation kind; cease wanting the audience's attention. Innocent Dada can own cruelty without supplying a complete lesson. Murderer Dada's claimed inert liquid and supposed agreement to a private live version must remain distinct from the empty-jacket film.

**Same-stage innocent Opportunity shape:**

> I wanted the screen on during his toast. I went into the private room to find the coordinator, showed her the falling-jacket film on my phone, and tried to restore the canceled cue. She said no again. Grant's place was set. I had such a good answer ready for her. Cruel. Very funny, I thought. I can't remember it now.

This is a sustained failed persuasion: desired cue, attempt, refusal, failure to retrieve the clever reply. The set place stays explicit without becoming the climax of the account. The coordinator's refusal and room visit are existing facts, not a newly staged exchange. The final failed recall is already printed; it does not require a new event or a listener to interrupt.

Keep innocent Method's wish for Grant alive, furious, and laughed at; the empty jacket; rejection; and continued pressure. Avoid a replacement coda about art's responsibility. Keep murderer Method's consent claim, canceled public program, extraction and inert belief, denial of rehearsing at the glass, and expectation that Grant would get up. The repetition of that expectation can carry the pressure; adding a witty metaphor would weaken it.

At murderer Finale, retain no live agreement, rejection record, preview poison and private-room dosing, actual killing, false reliance on the jacket film, removed authorship, and refusal of an artistic explanation. The last wish that Vincent would stop looking already reverses the original desire for an audience. This is one destination worth preserving rather than reinventing.

### 6. Robin: prevent an accusation against the advocate from ending the claim

**Action across the turns:** demand a hearing or intact return; take the petition into the disputed space despite warning; concede trespass or sampling while fighting over which location the room accepts; finally retain the demand after losing personal authority to speak for it. Innocent Robin stays argumentative. Murderer Robin must admit the claimants authorized none of the killing and that they now bear its consequences.

**Same-stage murderer Method shape:**

> I took a contents sample at the preview because I wanted proof of what this museum called harmless. I didn't take it into Grant's private room. The signature page went to the announcement desk. The interview was at the public podium; that's the exchange on Paige's recorder.

These are the same claims in the same turn. The concession is used to defend the boundary between sampling and the private room. There is no invitation to get an unprinted answer from Paige. This changes the argumentative action, but the late sampling admission is still late and conspicuous. Moving it to Opportunity would change disclosure timing and is forbidden here.

Retain the existing distinction between an intact return **or** hearing in the innocent demand and the wording already printed in the murderer ending. Do not silently standardize its and/or formulations. Keep the private-place recording, signature-page claim, preview sample, later poisoning, moved-location lie, the claimants' lack of authorization, and the harm to them. Preserve the existing phrase about putting poison in the glass afterwards. A new distraction during the interview would invent the missing contamination opportunity readers asked for.

The murderer can end on the unresolved bowl/hearing demand with the guilt and claimant harm already admitted. That ending is an attempt to keep a political subject alive after disqualifying oneself. It requires no promise that leadership will grant anything.

## Elle: the permitted adjustment and its evidentiary limit

Use wording to aim her request at ownership, money, and the unfinished obligation. Do not make exclusive custody of the cloth the recurring present-tense action. Keep her route clear and complete; do not bury it in unrelated emotional prose. Elle's Method sketch above is an example of reducing a misleading behavioral cue while retaining a fair opportunity.

Specifically avoid new statements such as: Chip helped roll it; everybody saw the panel empty; she never put it near the bottle; she held it open the entire time; no scrap could stick to it; her hands were full; she never touched the glass; the merchant caused the discrepancy. Each would change testimony or imply a material exoneration. The financial denial must continue to be her own false claim until its scheduled admission.

Do not have Elle explain that debt is unrelated to murder before the vote. That would turn the dialogue into the designer's ranking of legitimate suspicions. The physical description and the lie remain for players to assess. Removing guarded-roll wording may reduce one speculative transport narrative; it cannot establish that the innocent account is more likely than every competing murderer defense.

## What this scope can and cannot achieve

Different speech actions are feasible because these people already have different wants. A version with more sustained resistance, selective frankness, and unfinished desires could improve how the fixed material plays. It needs fresh evidence of reception. No numerical naturalness threshold or accusation distribution follows from this consultation.

An exclusive identification of one hand is a separate problem. Open access to the bottle, broad access to the waiting glass, a nonunique scrap, and competing documented lies remain the printed situation. Some innocent secondary lies are as objectively contradicted as murderer method lies. Prose can make the subjects of those lies clearer; it cannot make one physically impossible. The t002 result also shows that Elle's cloth language is not the sole source of the problem.

The fixed question rounds, repeated visits, and comprehensive documentary assembly will continue to make the game's construction visible to some readers. Replacing factual disclosure with improvised exchanges would change both performance procedure and evidence reliability. That route is outside the current brief.

My concrete recommendation is a bounded second prose candidate using these six contrasts, followed by a semantic review of the entire selected sequence before any further cohort. Do not add more evidence or reopen the release design in this pass. If the user's required outcome is a logically exclusive culprit in every scenario, report that objective as infeasible under the fixed-facts boundary; do not imply that better phrasing supplies the missing exclusion.

## Review before any new cohort

1. Compare every revised turn with its same-stage, same-branch obligations, including all small qualifiers. Confirm that nothing about means, timing, witnesses, routes, authorization, glass identity, documents, or knowledge moved or changed.
2. Read the selected whole-cast sequence aloud. For each turn, identify what the speaker tries to get from the room and whether the middle of the turn serves that effort. Remove generic final lessons and invented partner reactions. Check that several turns simply answer and stop.
3. Compare the innocent and murderer branches for unintended behavioral signals such as exclusive object custody. Do not make innocence systematically cooperative or guilt systematically evasive; preserve the stated factual differences and the specific person.
4. Keep evidence bytes and release ordering unchanged. Render affected boxes and check line breaks, page count, and accidental omission. Preserve candidate01's complete scores separately from the new version.
5. If authorized fresh staged readers run, give them only the new frozen player release, unchanged sequential gates, immutable scores and locked accusation before Finale. Use the same definitions for comparison. This consultant must not participate as a blind reader. Report a failure as a failure; do not combine favorable scores from the two texts.

The useful second pass therefore has two honest aims: make six or more people sustain different social actions through their actual turns, and remove the accidental invitation to treat innocent Elle's packing request as protection of concealed evidence. It leaves a recorded structural limit for any identity problem that survives those changes.
