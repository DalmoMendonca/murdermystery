Two anonymous versions of a read-aloud scene. Each character has two alternative role readings; these are not one playable case. Assess writing, not guilt or mystery solvability.

VERSION X

Dada DiCapo / Performance Artist & Cultural Celebrity

Alternative reading 1
An empty jacket. A chair. A thump. That was the studio film we delivered before tonight. Grant refused a live stunt with himself or any guest, then rejected the screen version too. I kept asking to show it. I wanted him embarrassed.

Alternative reading 2
The film rehearsed Grant's entrance. He agreed to fall after the private toast. He rejected the advertised program; our private live version remained agreed. I expected him to get up.

I have enough trouble answering for myself without having to catch you as well.

Tess Tament / Philanthropist & Heir to the Larceny Family Fortune

Alternative reading 1
