## Suspicious innocent access and fair proof

Retaining innocent access is desirable: it prevents solving by spotting the only person who mentions a glass. It means opportunity cannot be used as an elimination proof. The accusation should combine a threatened interest, credible source and glass access, and a material false account independently contradicted by an original exhibit. Allow guests to argue alternative suspects; do not claim every innocent is physically incapable.

A lying innocent can remain fair if the lie concerns an acknowledged scandal and does not contradict the selected murderer’s defining physical fact. Avoid assigning the same material Method lie to an innocent. Leave payments, relationships and professional consequences unsettled while keeping objective sample, photograph, recording and document facts stable. An unresolved debt is not a second killer.

The source-to-glass chain is strong circumstantial evidence, not witnessed chemical transfer. Neither cyanide residue nor similar red paper identifies one hand. Preserve explicit wording that stock is similar and has no unique torn-edge match. The later cupboard access means preview access is not logically exclusive; accusations must not call it the only possible extraction window.

## Human reading burden

Print a short shared timeline once: preview 6:20–6:28; bare crest glass set 6:32; collected 7:04; poured in kitchen 7:06; delivered 7:09. Name the glass, bottle, bowl and alcove consistently. Keep visual pairs together: physical bottle/wooden model, installed open bench/proposed glass front, foil paper/gold leaf, crest glass/sealed water.

Reference exhibits by printed title in spoken answers and accusation aids. One decisive contradiction plus the access/motive chain is sufficient; do not require recollection of every employment settlement. Preserve confession as consequence, not the first place a player learns an essential source opportunity.
