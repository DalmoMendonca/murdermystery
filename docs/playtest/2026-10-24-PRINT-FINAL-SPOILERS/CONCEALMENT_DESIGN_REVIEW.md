# Innocent concealment design review / SPOILERS

Nonblind review of PRINT-FINAL candidate-source, 2026-10-24. No source changes or reader tests performed.

The mechanism is fair and addresses a real structural weakness: the sole person who continues lying should not automatically be the murderer. Use six strong denials initially; nine is a ceiling, not a quota. A larger number creates more contradictory testimony for human guests to retain and may flatten every character into the same evasive voice. Pilot counts do not establish that every extra suspect needs a score above five.

The deciding distinction must be **what the lie conceals**. An innocent lies about a threatened job, improper authority or financial misconduct while supplying the true fact about the bottle/glass/service. A murderer supplies the false fact that removes a necessary part of the murder sequence. A denial of wrongdoing alone cannot prove murder. This is circumstantial deduction, not a formal guarantee that every other person was incapable.

Add a short player instruction before accusations: "People may conceal misconduct even if they did not kill Grant. Explain how your suspect's false account connects to the poison, the waiting glass, or its delivery." Do not tell guests which people have the innocent cover stories or identify every lie as it is spoken. Coming Clean resolves the separate scandals after votes.

## Important source and fairness checks

1. **Avoid denials that the source cannot prove false.** "I never meant to mislead," "I thought I had permission," "someone else changed it," and "I spent none of that advance" cannot be refuted by a correction, a missing approval attachment, two differing dates, or an advance-return receipt alone. Those are motives/interpretations, not objective contradictions. A stated absence of authorization must mean none existed, not merely no paper was found.
2. **Do not make innocent concealment contaminate the murder proof.** Chip tells the truth about foil paper; Cary about the used crest glass; Mona about sealed water; Al about actual materials. In each guilty world that character's defining Method falsehood still contradicts its independent physical/recorded proof. No innocent repeats that falsehood. No new poison handling or unique physical transfer is required.
3. **The lies must survive their own earlier speeches.** Neutral earlier wording is essential, particularly Cary and Mona. A character can change a story under pressure, but nine visible reversals would feel authored and give away the mechanism. Keep each innocent on one consistent cover story until Coming Clean.
4. **Confession cannot introduce missing pre-vote proof.** Guests see the independent scandal exhibit before the vote. Coming Clean explains responsibility and consequence. Otherwise they are asked to decide which lies matter with evidence they have not received.
5. **Protect accurate accusations.** A strong accusation names the threatened interest, credible bottle/glass access, the specific false account of the murder sequence, and its independent refutation. Do not promise mathematical uniqueness from access: multiple innocents have genuine access, and ordinary staff keys open the cupboard. Paper-stock similarity remains nonunique.
6. **Keep the delivery varied.** Cary flatly denies causation; Chip insists on his records; Monet invokes approval; Mona asserts authority; Al shifts contract responsibility; Anne minimizes who paid; Sue disputes the bundle; Penny minimizes unfairness; Justin rewrites his own publication history. These are different pressures, not nine repetitions of "I did nothing wrong."
7. **Do not guarantee heatmap gains.** This change may increase innocent suspicion, but a careful reader can still distinguish a financial or reputational lie from a lie that disables the poisoning mechanism. That distinction is the intended fair solve. Test whether guests can explain it, not merely whether they keep a fixed number of alternatives above a numeric threshold.

There is a separate existing continuity issue in the current candidate: Monet innocent Opportunity personally carries Grant's ticket, while Coming Clean says it went back through the manager. Repair the finale to reflect the actual innocent errand before printing. It does not affect the guilty timing refutation but would introduce accidental post-vote contradiction.
