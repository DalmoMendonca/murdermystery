# Fresh independent ensemble judge 02

**Verdict:** Playable and emotionally intelligible, but still more a succession of prepared depositions than a developing table scene. Naturalness **6.5/10**; arc variety **6/10**. Ready for a separate diagnostic fair-play test using the exact branch/cue procedure. Not ready for finished-ensemble certification. Forensic fairness is not certified.

## Scope and performed shape

I read only candidate05-stage-order.md: all 22 roles, all 176 passage alternatives and its procedure. I evaluated one murderer branch, 21 innocent branches, all 22 answering each of three rounds, and only named top-three innocents followed by the murderer at the finale. Alternatives are not consecutive speeches; all 22 confessions do not occur.

The procedure is clear in principle: choosing any marked member activates the complete fixed exchange; only its final speaker chooses an unheard outsider; the host marks the group heard when it finishes. Normal guest chaining continues elsewhere. The finale permits two innocent speeches when the culprit is in the top three, otherwise three, followed by the culprit. No fourth innocent replaces a waiting murderer. Most innocent emotional resolutions therefore never happen.

## Whole ensemble

Immediate practical wants are the strongest feature: Friday wages, coats and transport for caterers, a dependent waiting at home, packing a weave, moving a chair, an unanswered family call. Sue/Mona retain friendship through disagreement. Claire/Reed retain a recognition problem. Vincent's envy and Cary's request for another job expose relationships without requiring a finale. Grant plausibly pressures an institution through money, ownership and reputation.

Across 66 pre-vote answers, however, too many arcs follow the same progression: Grant attached humiliation to payment or legitimacy; I changed or withheld a document/object; now I distinguish records or deny an inconvenient fact. Individually convincing coercion becomes overconcentrated across the cast. Many guests also produce exceptionally polished diagnoses of their own failure. Method often becomes an exhibit index rather than frightened conversation.

Murderer opportunity alternatives repeatedly use private place, waiting glass, absent Grant. Only one murderer alternative is performed, but numerous innocents also encounter that prepared place. The room feels like a rotating witness station. Common access is legitimate mystery structure; recurring empty-chair staging weakens naturalness.

The limited exchanges help pacing more than reciprocity. Artie/Monet/Sue sustain a recognizable institutional dispute, though Sue's statement is largely inserted. Claire and Chip speak once before doctor/Reed takes over; the doctor's hold instruction and Reed's revenge explanation connect. Dada/Barb/Brie/Cary mostly provide adjacent evidence statements. More parts do not substantially change the other 18 arcs' prepared accounts.

Branch-sensitive handoffs are weakest in method. Murderer Barb asks Brie to check footage; innocent Brie's next line is the food-bill reminder. Innocent Barb releases staff at shift's end; murderer Brie explains the funded drink order. Neither is impossible, but neither convincingly responds. An improvised reply must not be assumed to repair either.

## Compatibility for any culprit

I found no definite world-breaking contradiction remaining after allowing culprit lies and the innocent lies their conditional finales admit. Innocent Elon calling an invoice an estimate and innocent Cary denying damage need not become true when another person is the culprit. Evidentiary disagreement is not automatically a continuity failure.

Each of the 22 culprit alternatives eventually supplies extraction/poisoning and a particular false pre-vote explanation. I did not infer that all guests privately knew cyanide was present, that unnamed witnesses corroborate visits, or that direct addresses secure answering admissions. Monet allegedly calling Elon's proposal a bill is an asserted past dispute, not a response supplied by the schedule.

This is speech-level compatibility only. Access, timing, contradiction and a uniquely supportable accusation need separate testing in every culprit world. Finales cite records not reproduced as exhibits here; mentioning them cannot establish their availability or evidentiary quality.

## Must-fixes and conventions

Before claiming ensemble readiness, make method-exchange continuations work after either preceding branch without invented dialogue or historical facts. Rehearse cue activation with ordinary readers: a chosen mid-group member triggers the full schedule, nobody chooses next early, and the host tracks groups and the remaining chain. This is an operational verification need.

For literary readiness, vary the remaining single-turn progression and give more guests a distinct action or relationship movement by round three. Do not depend on innocent finales: 18 or 19 innocents receive none. Recognizable movement does not require universal redemption or an epilogue for everyone.

Acceptable conventions include pun names, shared questions, compromising secrets, factual contradiction, documentary verification and confession after locked voting. Equal conversational airtime is unnecessary. The weakness is aggregate sameness and the gap between a printed exchange and a response.
