## Safe variation

Vary cadence, sentence shape, interruptions within the same speaking turn, the length of a pause, and a speaker's existing emotional emphasis. Existing requests can become more playable requests addressed to the same person, with the same object and intended action. The baseline already supplies host anxiety, teacher/student strain, artists' credit, staff wages, frightened sources, home obligations, and institutional shame: develop their rhetoric while keeping every factual assertion and its certainty. A rhetorical question must remain a question; an unreturned request cannot acquire a reply.

An address to Claire, Chip, Brie, Dada or another player is a relationship cue. It does not establish that person's attendance, private knowledge, agreement, reaction or corroboration. A new nod, flinch, interrupted denial, handled document or exchanged glance can become a clue. Prefer changes in the current speaker's expression over invented stage business involving another player.

## Claims cannot migrate freely

Keep each claim in its original branch and round. Motive establishes threats and personal stakes; Opportunity establishes handling/access and the account of a visit; Method commits to the disputed account; finales correct and explain after the vote. Moving a Method lie to another speaker removes ownership of the falsification. Moving a finale's correction earlier erases the lie or reveals the confession. Adding an earlier explanation of a later contradiction changes inference even when all eventual facts remain somewhere in the script.

Particularly sensitive ownership: Claire authorizes intake; Chip fits the band; Art writes the dated query; Reed edits the press copy; Barb orders camera power removal; Elon offers a sample panel; Brie handles individual flavoring; Cary hands over the sealed bottle and gift carton; Anne leads the actual tour; Paige owns the interview/export chain; Robin owns the petition intervention; Justin knows what his clipping correction actually changes. Giving their firsthand assertions to friends or rivals creates unearned witnesses. Asking a person to fetch a record does not mean the person already knows its contents.
