# Three bounded exchange proposals

This is an author draft over `candidate03-source/confirmed.yaml`, read together with the complete `candidate03-stage-order.md`. It preserves the earlier drafts. No release, packet, host PDF, source bank or finale has been edited. It is ready for independent semantic and whole-ensemble review, not accepted on the author's judgment.

The actual host procedure lets a guest select the next unheard guest. The analytical stage-order export does not establish a fixed performance order. These exchanges therefore require a facilitator schedule and matching packet directions for three groups. They cannot be implemented by quietly splitting boxes under the existing chain rule.

## Cues and file shape

`draft-exchanges.yaml` has three schedules under `metadata`, with one-based group indices and explicit `{id, part}` turns. Its `fields` mapping contains complete numbered speech objects (`part`, `speech`) for both branches of every member of each scheduled group. Part numbers, names and schedule cues are not spoken. Including the unsplit group members makes the proposed sequence reviewable in one file; their speech is copied from candidate03.

The facilitator calls the listed parts consecutively for each scheduled group. Guests read only their selected branch and wait when their part ends. A returning guest reads the next numbered part, not their whole answer again. The facilitator releases the group before the ordinary unheard-guest chain resumes. The parent owns the precise placement of these blocks within the act and any necessary packet or host prototype. No new question or improvised reply is required. First entries retain the listed order within each group.

Only six roles acquire a second entry: Artie and Monet in Act I, Dr. Fact and Reed in Act II, Barb and Brie in Act III. This adds two entries in each act. The other sixteen roles keep one turn in that act; no general two-part format is applied.
