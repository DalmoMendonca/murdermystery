For each dispute, specify one actual consequence of Grant's proposed deal, earlier history, a limited number of events at tonight's reception, and competing character agendas. A role must connect to another guest's action, not solely to Grant. Do not make all characters accomplices or require any absent guest to deliver an essential fact. Master and attending editions need explicit dependency checks.

Write the crime history and investigation disclosures separately. Log what each statement adds at each release; a confession repeated in another act earns no place. Establish opportunity through relevant acts, observation, access or interrupted plans, not a thirty-person synchronized alibi schedule. Move historical arguments to their actual earlier dates instead of having Grant personally confront everyone between arrival and the toast.

Before more full dialogue, draft ten distinct unresolved murder hypotheses across the six disputes. Each needs existing motive, a concrete act that reasonably bears on the source or target, and a later innocent interpretation tied to that person's actual agenda. Reject constructions that require everybody to touch the bottle, move the same glass, carry matching cloths or obtain an individual certificate. Innocent context can reduce suspicion without pretending it physically proves impossibility. The guilty branch must retain a positive pre-vote case and its confession must explain, rather than introduce, the identifying facts.

First test these compact premises for ordinary plausibility and timeline feasibility, including the quieter selectable roles. Then write a small connected scene set with actual named questions. Compare the two branches for disclosure, voice and form before expanding. No new score trial until this ledger and scene audit pass. No identical reruns seeking favorable scores. A later primary trial still must meet the original breadth, timing and final-alternative requirements; none is waived.

Artifact work remains downstream: replacement images must show the actual facts, every final page must be visually reviewed, and the pinned public fallback may be replaced only by a verified complete kit.
