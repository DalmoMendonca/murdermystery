# Two-story foundation — feasibility draft

Not player copy, not accepted, and not a new tested edition. It preserves the public cast. Its purpose is to settle what the mystery is about before another dialogue rewrite.

## The crime

Grant is about to announce a gift that consolidates his control over the museum and exposes several private arrangements. One invited character decides to stop him. The historical poison is an opportunity, not a profession-specific skill: its acquisition and retained contents are known through Grant's display campaign and the auction condition. The culprit secretly enters preparation storage and steals the original. No innocent needs to lend them poison or knowingly help.

The culprit uses a common miniature to carry the fictional poison, contaminates Grant's empty private-toast glass, and abandons the original among returns. Service later fills the glass from the shared batch. Grant dies at the gala. The common chemical and glass findings establish this route, while leaving the carrier's personal ownership open. No real poison quantities or handling instructions belong in the kit.

The actual event has several independent innocent disruptions. Artie holds the private setting while trying to stop the announcement; Brie refuses replacement and withdraws cover; Monet prolongs the kitchen dispute; security moves unexamined returns; collections and installation workers conceal their own errors. Journalists and claimants interfere with Grant's papers and pictures. Their goals collide and create the vulnerability. They are not all secretly assisting murder.

This core still needs a plausible security response: the missing original cannot be treated as harmless simply because the donor says so without consequences. Staff negligence must have clear, different causes and later accountability. It must not require every worker to behave identically or ignore an obvious threat just to permit the crime.

## The investigation guests experience

**Introductions:** meet the museum's awkward coalition. Each person wants an actual decision tonight: signature, payment, credit, withdrawal, publication or return of property. The questions build relationships and motives rather than introduce30 separate unexplained scandals.

**Hunt:** recover16 short artifacts from those disputes. They establish the gift, ownership claims, source hazard, program and ordinary service arrangements. No artifact instructs players which clue matters. The playing host receives essential unfound evidence through the scheduled evidence releases, without a separate solution catalog.

**Motive:** the apparent benefactor was using the announcement against people. Guilty and innocent accounts admit comparable pressure. Nobody is identified merely because one motive paragraph is darker or longer.

**Opportunity:** participants explain consequential acts, not synchronized alibis. At least five core rivals have a specific potential crime route. Additional cases should make eight of22 and ten of30 credible without inventing ten poison administrations. Core acts need staff records or material consequences, not just self-accusation.

**Method:** investigators identify source, carrier and target and test the accounts. Physical construction, drink provenance and material comparison can turn ordinary earlier claims into contradictions. The culprit's account should fit less well than the innocent interpretation, while three or four serious rival cases remain imperfectly resolved. Method must not give almost everyone a complete innocence certificate while one speaker says little.

**Accusations / Coming Clean:** players vote from what has already been revealed. The top three read the appropriate branch; if necessary the murderer stands afterward. Innocent endings resolve their scandals and the rival interpretations. The confession explains the same planted crime and cover, adding drama rather than the first identifying evidence.

## Proposed shared fact constraints

These are design candidates, not claims that current player files already contain them.

- **C1 / source construction:** the supplied handling copy has an open bottom; recovered original has a closed base and fresh neck damage. A claim that a known copy contained liquid needs examination. Empty does not itself mean open-bottomed; do not conflate the two facts.
- **C2 / drink provenance:** the shared toast batch is delivered to reception only when Casey brings the collected glass back on the service tray. An ordinary delivery slip and the first-pour photograph establish this in Method. A claim of filling the particular glass from that batch at the reception bar beforehand is then testable. This changes the workflow and requires a new early delivery-order plant and compatible staff accounts. It is not the old claim that an empty-looking photograph proves continuous emptiness.
- **C3 / material comparison:** source-neck fragments join the original and cannot come from supplied miniatures; intact prop/other retained items may answer a specific broken-item claim. Mixed waste never establishes individual ownership by itself. A role needs a naturally linked action at the target as well as a material discrepancy.

C2 has a plausible service cause, but fails the accusation check in `CASE_PROOF.md`: a false drink-provenance claim can conceal an unpaid or unauthorized drink without identifying the poisoner. C1 and C3 also fail individual attribution. None of these three constraints is approved as the next foundation. Do not expand them into full dialogue or spend on blind tests.

## Architecture decisions still required

No one constraint is a universal30-role solution. The30 short premises in `cover-premises.yaml` distinguish promising source-work roles from weak visitor material excuses and potentially workable hospitality covers. None is accepted. Reject a premise if it needs another blue prop, irrelevant bottle accident, incidental glass move, magical late witness or personalized host-selected exhibit.

Before implementation: specify two independently planted grounds for each selected case; write its credible innocent counterstory; ensure all staff facts stay true in every selected world; verify that any necessary clue survives reserve absence. If a counterstory predicts the evidence just as well, strengthen the causal scene or replace the cover family before spending on a blind reviewer.

## Feasibility decision

**Rejected as an architecture for the next edition.** The thirty entries expose role-fit limits; they do not establish thirty working covers. See `CASE_PROOF.md` for the three actual counterfactual walkthroughs and `foundation-decision.json` for the machine-readable gate result. Retain the agendas as character research. Do not retain the proposed object errands merely because they now have names attached.
