# Foundation rejection: three counterfactual walkthroughs

October 8, 2026. Author-only analysis of proposals, not new player evidence, a blind review, or a tested edition. No source packet has changed. This is the inexpensive rejection gate the previous process needed.

## What constitutes progress here

Finding a contradiction is insufficient. The accusation must explain why that contradiction favors murder over the person's independently motivated misconduct. Two observations of one disputed act are not two independent grounds. A contradiction must also be attributable to the speaker, rather than to unidentified mixed waste. A rival story must be evaluated before the confession, without assuming that every innocent statement is guaranteed true.

The following cases use the proposed common constraints in `TWO_STORIES.md`. Those constraints have not been put into the kit. Each case fails without adding a new contrivance.

## 1. Chip: an original mistaken for a copy

**Accusation:** Chip has source knowledge, removes case lining after being told to preserve it, and falsely calls a liquid-retaining object the handling copy. The examined copy has an open bottom. Chip therefore handled the original, concealed that handling, and had the means to poison Grant.

**Credible competing account:** Chip mishandled the original while trying to conceal an altered conservation record. Another guest stole or used its contents afterward. Removing the lining explains a real professional concealment without explaining administration to Grant. Neither the lining nor the object's capacity puts Chip at the contaminated glass.

**What the evidence actually establishes:** source handling and concealment may be supported; administration is not. The construction comparison and the liquid-retaining claim are parts of the same discrepancy. They are not independent source and target links.

**Decision:** reject C1 as a sufficient identity mechanism. Do not add a glass-cleaning errand to Chip, then repeat it for the other ten C1 roles. A conservation scene can remain a strong competing source case, but it is not a complete selectable-culprit solution.

## 2. Anne: a drink offered to postpone the announcement

**Accusation:** Anne says she brought Grant the reception punch before Casey collected the glass. Under C2 that batch had not reached reception. She had a reason to delay the donor's announcement and admits contact with his drink. Her explanation is false, and the individual glass contains poison while the shared punch is clear.

**Credible competing account:** Anne obtained a different drink and called it the official punch to avoid explaining an unauthorized interruption. Or she mistakenly identified the batch. Even an intentional false claim about the drink's origin does not establish that she stole the historical poison. The poisoned glass could have been contaminated before her intervention.

**What the evidence actually establishes:** a delivery record could contradict provenance if her claim is sufficiently specific. It does not establish the glass's entire previous history or the person's source access. Adding exact batch wording solely to make the lie catchable would make a quiet educator sound like a forensic deposition.

**Decision:** reject C2 as a general visitor solution. For Brie, responsibility for drink preparation makes the scene more plausible, but a false preparation account still needs a separate grounded connection to deliberate contamination. Do not make fourteen different people fetch Grant a drink to satisfy the same test.

## 3. Justin: a miniature supposedly broken with the phone protector

**Accusation:** Justin personally claims to have broken his dark-blue miniature and wrapped its pieces with his damaged phone protector. The dark-blue pieces recovered from waste fit the historical original, not a supplied miniature. Justin admits obstructing clearance of Grant's setting. The carrier and target contain the same poison.

**Credible competing account:** Justin broke an unexamined bottle, misidentified pieces he gathered, or lied about an unrelated unauthorized item. The waste was combined. The original's fragments and poisoned miniature are not individually assigned to him. His obstruction puts him near the target but does not show that he administered poison. The damaged phone genuinely corroborates part of his story in both branches.

**What the evidence actually establishes:** a material mismatch challenges the precise breakage account, conditional on individual fragment custody. That custody is missing. The only blue-bottle breakage allocated to G is also a narrative spotlight: readers can select the speaker before doing the supposed comparison.

**Decision:** reject C3 as the next thirty-role foundation. Do not repair it by giving painters, photographers and critics conveniently breakable blue objects. Preserve the real publication dispute; discard the imposed miniature accident as a universal cover.

Additional anger, forgery and threats cannot turn that into eight credible poisoners by themselves. No suspicion score is assigned or guaranteed here.

## Result and change in approach

The all-thirty audit failed: **zero premises accepted**. Three were already explicitly rejected for invented material; the remaining twenty-seven do not become feasible merely by being labeled "needs causal proof." The new C1/C2/C3 architecture must not enter another full transcript run.

Keep the setting, sent character identities, secret animal selection, three acts and hunt. Retain the minimum intelligible crime facts: a known historical source is missing, Grant's individual drink is poisoned, and the shared drink is clear. Reconsider the compulsory miniature/fragment/replica transfer chain rather than requiring every character to explain it. An exhibit can establish a crime mechanism without being the universal identifying device.

The next implementation must start with one short confrontation around a character's real decision, plus its innocent and guilty versions. The final contradiction must arise from that decision and an independently planted fact. Choose contrasting roles before expansion. Reject a scene if the innocent version has no reason to occur or if the guilty version alone is assigned every crime object. There must be actual question context and a rival interpretation, not just a new three-family label.

No next version number or blind test is warranted yet. This rejection is a concrete execution result of the new gate, not evidence that the game's balance improved.
