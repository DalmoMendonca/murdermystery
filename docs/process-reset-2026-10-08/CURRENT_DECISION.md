## What was wrong with the process

The iterations adjusted evidence timing and dialogue before settling whether the
story's core construction was natural. Numerical tests encouraged tuning toward a
suspect count while repeated handlers, synchronized alibis, and clearance records
made the story visibly engineered. Reviewer agreement was sometimes allowed to
stand in for gameplay measurement. Neither is enough.

The current repair found a subtler version of the same problem: only guilty Method
speeches narrated an elaborate earlier encounter with the crime object. Fixing the
question wording alone did not fix the signal. The independent editor rejected it.

## Execution sequence and spending limits

1. **Causal foundation:** examine all thirty agendas and both branches against one
   fixed event history. A scene must advance a character's own dispute and weaken
   or support a particular accusation. Do not require thirty airtight alibis.
2. **Matched speech gate:** both branches must answer the same actual question in
   comparable voices and forms. A murderer cannot be detectable merely as the only
   person introducing a vivid crime-object scene or giving an evasive answer.
   Apply this check before writing the remaining twenty-three full pairs.
3. **Limited independent review:** one bounded review of representative difficult
   pairs, not four simultaneous full transcripts. Archive the exact reviewed bytes.
   A rejection identifies a mechanism to repair; it does not earn another score run.
4. **One staged blind trial:** only after a full playable bank exists. Predeclare
   order, thresholds, and culprit selection; conceal the selection from the reader.
   Stop after Act II if suspicion breadth or naturalism fails. Continue through
   Act III only if the middle gate passes. No rerun of identical bytes seeking a
   favorable score. A promising complete result can earn one replication.
5. **Architecture limit:** at most two tested candidates for a causal construction.
   If both fail, change that construction and document why; do not issue another
   lightly edited numbered variant. No PDF production or publication until story,
   every-culprit continuity, and balance gates pass.
