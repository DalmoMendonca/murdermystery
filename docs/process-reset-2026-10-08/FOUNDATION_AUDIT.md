# Foundation audit — shared facts and missing arguments

This is the first execution step of the process reset. It does not introduce new player facts or claim a solved design.

## Existing physical argument

1. The historical original contains the fictional poison, is intact when secured, and is stolen after forced opening.
2. Its recovered neck is damaged. A contaminated fragment joins it.
3. An intact miniature from common favor stock contains the same poison.
4. Poison is inside the donor-specific glass; sampled shared punch and named alternative materials are clear for that poison.
5. The glass is photographed empty at placement and collection, then filled on a staff tray. Residue during the unattended interval is feasible.

This supports a source–carrier–target mechanism. It does **not** identify who stole, transferred or administered it. The original, miniature and waste have no unique personal ownership chain. Clear samples do not establish every person's whereabouts or all possible conduct.

## Identity has to enter through the variable testimony

With identical exhibits in every selected world, identity must arise from branch-specific claims, observations or a tested contradiction. That is compatible with the host-blind format. A different personal-paper match in each world is not compatible. The essential question is whether the guilty account loses against the evidence for a natural reason, rather than because it alone is allocated the crime objects or omitted all corroboration.

Current relocation prototypes fail that test of dramatic symmetry. Round23 changes the selecting detail to a miniature-breakage claim. It improves the physical comparison but may still be a bespoke G-only incident. Its cause and carrier custody remain incomplete. No new scores justify treating it as the foundation for all30.

## Competing cases must explain an actual crime

A missing source, a specific contaminated target and deliberate concealment are different hypotheses. The new service and packing scenes can contribute, but a false service order or an unpaid invoice is not automatically an administration case. For each core rival the next bible must state:

- the particular act that could cause Grant's death;
- the independently planted fact supporting that act;
- the innocent scene that predicts the same observation;
- the later fact changing its significance;
- the suspicion that legitimately remains afterward.

If a character's only evidence is anger plus possible presence, label the case weak. Do not make it stronger with arbitrary extra blue glass, a second perfume mishap or a synchronized document pickup.

## Thirty-role feasibility remains open

The new physical architecture has no accepted all30 set. A complete set of fields and a confession are insufficient. Every proposed G must have plausible source knowledge/access and a target intervention; every I must retain a meaningful mitigation. All fixed staff observations must still be true when a core participant is selected G. Reserve absence cannot remove the only necessary witness.

The next deliverable is30 short cover premises against one shared timeline, grouped only for audit efficiency. Family members still need distinct agendas and false accounts. Infeasible premises are rejected before full dialogue. The implementation may reuse the murder mechanism; it may not disguise one repeated excuse as30 distinct stories.

## Naturalism and playability checks

The investigator can compare evidence after rounds, but actors should pursue their gala goals while replying. The story needs consequences between roles: an order changes service, a dispute changes what staff clear, a concealment changes a public announcement. Staff records should arise because workers need to finish their jobs. Avoid multiplying records solely to keep eight names above a threshold.

Packets need their real named question context and realistic read-aloud length. Hunt papers reveal scene context and evidence; essential deductions must also be available if an envelope is missed. Finale resolves the same planted story, rather than supplies an unseen fact making the accusation possible.

**Next gate:** root must be able to explain the actual crime and the competing false stories in plain language before commissioning another blind reader. No new candidate is accepted by this document.
