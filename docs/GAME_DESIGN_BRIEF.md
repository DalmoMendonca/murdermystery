# The Last Acquisition - Game Design Brief

## Fixed design constraints

- Date: October 30, 2026; Tulsa, Oklahoma.
- Contemporary museum gala, fictional Meridian Museum of Art & World Cultures.
- Exhibition: **Treasures of the World**, modeled on an eclectic Met-like art/world-cultures institution.
- Tone: grounded serious mystery with heightened/eccentric characters and light quips; humor should mostly emerge from guest improvisation, not overwritten comedy.
- Victim: offstage NPC Sterling Voss, controversial billionaire donor with business, provenance, political-influence, and family-dynasty baggage.
- Cause: poisoning. Human murderer and human motive; ominous/superstitious atmosphere may exist only as misdirection.
- Attendance architecture: 15 core + 10 secondary + 5 tertiary roles; tiers invisible to players.
- Murderer selection: host-blind animal draw on party night; all 15 core roles eligible. Secondary/tertiary packets still include murderer branches so no role looks second-rate.
- Facilitator is also a full player and should not know the murderer.
- Runtime: ~2.5 hours of active game inside a 6:00-9:30 PM buffet dinner party.
- Act I: free-for-all scavenger hunt through the house-as-museum; discovered clues become public. Light puzzles only.
- Acts II-III: social deduction and character performance; minimal mandatory read-aloud.
- Final: ballots for murderer + evidence + motive, Best Actor, Best Costume; top three accused read final statements. If killer is not top three, killer wins Escaped Justice and then reveals/confesses.
- Difficulty target: medium. Bad outcome if nobody solves it; also bad if > half solve it.
- No phones/QR codes. Physical paper, envelopes, props, placards, ballots.
- Home spaces: open kitchen/dining/living; plus laundry, media room, guest bedroom, bathroom, Dalmo office, Viv office for scavenger hunt.
- Dinner: buffet on island/counter, assigned long-table seating; seating itself has no game logic.
- Cast: mostly characters in their 20s/30s with a few older outliers; roughly 70% elite museum/art-world roles, 30% staff/outsiders.
- Names: subtle-to-clearly-playful pun names preferred; avoid cartoon excess.
- Pre-party packet: name, description, personality, public background, a few known relationships, costume ideas; absolutely no Sterling relationship/motive/secret.
- Party-night packet: compact, skimmable, bullets where possible. One meaningful complication plus limited social objectives; secrets must serve main mystery rather than compete with it.
- Lying rules: innocents may evade/omit/deflect but not fabricate facts; murderer may lie; explicit reveal/submit instructions are mandatory.
- Content boundaries: no suicide, pregnancy/miscarriage, addiction, abuse, or racism. Romance/flirting/exes are fine but keep PG-13 and avoid explicit affairs.
- Cultural handling: real cultural regions + fictional specific artifacts; no ethnicity/nationality as punchline. Accents optional.
- Production: home printer for most materials; cheap creative props okay; 1-2+ hours setup is acceptable.
- Accessibility: no known special mobility/accessibility constraints.

## Quality bar

The game should feel like a high-quality commercial kit, but more bespoke: continuity-first, fair deduction, attendance-resilient clues, equal-feeling roles, host blindness, and clean printable assets. Every future edit should be checked against those invariants.
