## Initial generation (discarded visual variant)

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Project asset: a landscape documentary museum collections receiving photograph for a fictional museum-gala murder mystery, high resolution, realistic rather than a dramatic marketing illustration. One CLOSED opaque archival secondary case, the size of a small nineteenth-century bottle, on a clean cream conservation worktable. The case is pale gray rigid archival board with a fitted lid, securely tied with undisturbed cotton tape, and a small blank cream receiving tag attached. Behind it is the open outer steel preparation cabinet, with a normal undamaged keyed lock visible at the edge. The photograph must clearly show that the opaque secondary case is closed and intact and the cabinet is real secured museum storage. Neutral soft task lighting, authentic materials and subtle wear, restrained near-white background for economical print, sharp clear object details, natural photographic shadows. No readable text or numbers, no decorative border, no people, no bottle visible, no blue glass, no fragments, no chemicals or liquid, no skull symbols, no blood, no torn seals or forced lock. No forensic result or timestamp baked into image; those will be typeset separately. This is a BEFORE-THEFT condition photo, never a picture of the later recovery. Uncluttered editorial-quality photograph that can occupy a wide top half of a Letter-size evidence document.

## Targeted edit (selected)

Edit target: the attached receiving-condition museum photograph. Keep the same realistic conservation room, cream worktable, open steel cabinet, lighting, framing and uncluttered documentary photographic style. Replace ONLY the tall central ribbon-tied cardboard box with a small closed opaque pale-gray rigid protective collections storage case, about the same height, made of matte hard polypropylene. It has a fitted hinged lid, an ordinary rubber gasket seam and two closed metal latches, with one undisturbed plain tamper strip crossing its lid seam. Small blank cream receiving tag attached to one latch. The central item should read as secure protective museum storage, never as a gift box. Remove every cotton ribbon, bow and gift wrapping from that central case. Case closed and intact; no content visible, no bottle visible, no blue glass, no fragment, no readable text/numbers, no chemical handling instructions, no injury, no damaged lock or broken seal. Preserve all other scene elements. This is a fictional artifact's BEFORE-THEFT receiving photo, with later labels and timestamp to be professionally typeset outside the photograph.

The first variant looked like gift packaging. The edit replaced it with a rigid closed protective case. The photograph deliberately shows the receiving station before the cabinet is closed, not a locked cabinet condition. No bottle, blue source glass, fragment, timestamp, or identifying guest is visible.
