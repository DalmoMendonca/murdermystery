# Prospective round 18 test protocol

Written before any round 18 reader is dispatched. Do not edit this protocol after the inputs are frozen.

Eight saved stages retain the original order: introductions, sixteen Hunt discoveries, Motive evidence, Motive statements, Opportunity evidence, Opportunity statements, Method evidence, Method statements. Each score file is separately hash-validated before the next read. No earlier score can be changed. All trials are retained and any protocol exception is reported.

The three main trials repeat the originally random round 15 selections and attending casts, with fresh readers. This permits a controlled content comparison, not a claim of controlled reader judgment. A fourth fresh reader uses byte-identical trial B inputs with the original unanchored 0–10 instruction. It is an explicitly supplemental comparison, not a silent replacement for a failing main trial.

## Main-reader suspicion anchors

Suspicion describes the strength of a case worth pursuing. It is not a percentage probability and scores across characters need not sum to anything. Readers may use intervening integers and should not assign scores by a formula.

- **0:** Affirmative evidence excludes the character within the game's stated premise.
- **2:** Very little connects the character to the murder.
- **4:** A plausible motive or opportunity, but little concrete connection to the crime.
- **6:** Multiple specific circumstances connect the character to the crime; a credible investigative target with important links unresolved.
- **8:** The strongest or a very strong causal case, including a material contradiction or corroborated link; reasonable alternatives remain.
- **10:** Confession or an effectively conclusive case on the information presented.

These anchors must not mention desired counts, culprit identity, earlier ratings, final alternative targets, or a preferred curve. Brief reasons must state the actual evidence. A numerical pass without convincing reasons and natural story judgments does not establish acceptance.

## Comparison and interpretation

Earlier round scores remain untouched. The changed definition makes direct numerical comparisons with older unanchored trials limited. The supplemental unanchored reader helps distinguish a content improvement from a calibration effect. Report both methods honestly, including disagreement.

The actual party premise is exactly one murderer among the listed playing characters, no accomplices or offstage killer. No guarantee that innocent printed statements are truthful is supplied. Test readers must weigh claims against the exhibits and the other statements.

Naturalism, role continuity, early authorial tells, specificity of the final inference, and surviving credible alternatives are evaluated independently of numeric thresholds. No human solve rate can be estimated from these full-transcript readers.
