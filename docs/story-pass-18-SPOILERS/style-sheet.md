# Private dialogue style sheet

Derived from the already-sent acting tips in `source/character_copy.yaml`, not new public character traits. This is a working audit guide, not approved sample prose or an instruction to copy a formula.

American spelling throughout. Accessible names for objects: glass, punch, preparation cabinet, gift bottle. No coupe, communal cordial, coded chemical lot, or unexplained forensic acronym in spoken copy. Questions address people by name; readers never say an IF heading aloud.

This is a comic museum gala with consequences. Let characters pursue payment, authority, credit, ownership, careers, and relationships. A joke should arise from their immediate purpose. Do not end every admission with a tidy ethical reflection. Some answers can be clipped, evasive, irritated, or unfinished. Some speakers are precise and composed; that should not become the voice of the entire room.

## Review method

Read the lines with the names covered. Do they differ in how they answer, deflect, interrupt, and treat another person? Occupational nouns alone are insufficient. Preserve a character's intentional fragment or long sentence; do not mechanically alternate lengths to satisfy a count.

Every private edit must preserve the observed events and knowledge. Voice polish cannot repair a causal contradiction. Before freezing, check who knows the source contents, which surface is the sideboard, when a guest had access, and whether a claimed laboratory result is actually in the exhibits.

The newly installed Line Editing and Voice & Style checklists are applied by reading to this existing YAML/PDF project. Their novel-manuscript CLI scaffolding is not a replacement for the user's editable kit pipeline. Heavy private rewriting was explicitly authorized; public canon remains locked.
